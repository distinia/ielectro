Our Story

Our story began in 2018 as an idea shared by a small group of passionate coders, myself included. We were inspired by the power of technology to transform, and "iElectro" became our name to reflect that electric energy.

In 2024, I officially established iElectro as a startup while studying Computer Engineering at the University of Bergamo. My vision was clear: to create innovative tech solutions that empower and connect people, turning iElectro from a shared dream into a platform for impactful technology.
