The United States of Valmirica, commonly known as Valmirica or simply the United States, is a country located in northern Edrobe, encompassing an immense landmass of over 5.4 million square kilometers and home to a population exceeding 121 million inhabitants. Its capital and largest city is Montigon, a global center of politics, commerce, and culture. Valmirica stands as one of the world’s most influential nations—an economic, military, and ideological superpower.

The country operates under a federal presidential republic, with a directly elected President serving as both head of state and head of government, currently Robert Whitaker; a bicameral Congress composed of the Senate and House of Representatives; and an independent judiciary headed by the Supreme Court of Valmirica. The nation’s political system is a constitutional multiparty democracy, though in practice dominated by the Conservative Party and the Liberal Party, whose ideological rivalry has defined Valmirican politics for decades. The federal structure divides the republic into fifteen states, each with its own constitution and government, bound under the supremacy of the national charter.

Economically, Valmirica possesses one of the largest and most technologically advanced economies in the world, with a GDP of 4.5 trillion DTR, ranking second in Edrobe after Destenia. It is a highly industrialized, post-capitalist society driven by aerospace, robotics, digital technologies, renewable energy, and advanced manufacturing, supported by powerful research institutions and a stable financial system centered on the regional Valmirican Dollar (VRD). Despite its capitalist foundation, Valmirica maintains extensive social welfare systems, guaranteeing universal healthcare, education, and labor rights, reflecting its philosophy of liberty balanced by social responsibility.

Demographically, Valmirica is a highly urbanized, educated, and developed society, with over 82 percent of its population living in cities and a Human Development Index exceeding 0.94. The median age of 39.8 years reflects a balanced and dynamic population supported by advanced healthcare and education. The majority ethnic group, Valmiricans, make up 87 percent of the population, while the remaining 13 percent consists of immigrants and descendants from other Edrobean and Tayanusan nations, including Comussania, Boravia, Jarnovia, Agaritia, Cusea, Lamberia, and Ricene. The official language is Valmirican, while the population is predominantly Christian (82 percent), with recognized minorities of irreligious (15 percent) and Muslim (3 percent) citizens. Religion in Valmirica plays a cultural rather than political role, coexisting with a firmly secular constitutional order.

On the world stage, Valmirica is a founding member of the World Union, as well as an active participant in the World Trade Network, Global Reserve Fund, International Tribunal for Justice, and International Energy Agency. It identifies itself as the defender of democracy and human rights, exercising considerable influence through diplomacy, humanitarian aid, and cultural power. The Valmirican Armed Forces, among the most advanced on the planet, operate across land, sea, air, cyber, and space domains, maintaining a doctrine of strategic deterrence and global readiness. Its closest ally is Comussania, with which it shares deep economic and cultural ties, while its principal ideological rival is Destenia, representing an opposing model of totalitarian democracy and sovereign nationalism.

Valmirica’s vast geography spans from the frozen peaks of the Gelmaric Highlands and the glacial wilderness of Elgorath to the fertile Ravellan Plains and northern coastal fjords, encompassing one of the most diverse ecosystems in Edrobe. Its natural wealth, coupled with centuries of technological innovation, has made it both self-sufficient and globally integrated. Today, the United States of Valmirica stands as a beacon of constitutional democracy, economic power, and scientific progress, embodying the enduring belief that liberty is the source of strength, and strength the guardian of liberty—a nation forged in valor, sustained by unity, and destined to remain at the forefront of human advancement.

Etymology

The name Valmirica derives from a fusion of linguistic and ideological roots that reflect the country’s foundational identity. The prefix "Val-" is believed to originate from the archaic Valmirican word *valen*, meaning “strength” or “valor,” evoking ideals of courage, power, and resistance. This term was widely used in pre-constitutional revolutionary texts to signify both military determination and moral integrity, two concepts that would later become central to the national ethos.

The suffix "-mirica" is more contested in origin, but the prevailing scholarly interpretation links it to the phrase *mirica terra*, a poetic construction found in the independence-era declaration literature meaning “land of peace” or “land of unity.” Some theorists suggest it was derived from the word *mir*, meaning peace in several ancient Litherian dialects, fused with *rica*, an old Tayanusan loanword for realm or domain. Thus, Valmirica would literally mean “realm of strength and peace,” a symbolic contradiction that underscores the country’s identity as a nation forged through conflict but striving for liberty and stability.

The term Valmirican was first formalized in the Proclamation of Montigon, the day the Constitution was ratified. During this period, early revolutionary leaders debated between several names for the new federal union, including New Montigia, United Realms, and Common Federation, but Valmirica emerged as the most ideologically resonant term—one that combined the revolutionary fervor for self-determination with a broad unifying vision.

Culturally, the name Valmirica came to embody not only a geopolitical entity but also an entire civilizational project grounded in liberty, pluralism, and strength of will. The country’s motto, "Strength Through Liberty," echoes the dual heritage of its name—valor as the force behind freedom, and unity as its enduring goal. Linguistically, the name also served a strategic function: unlike many nation-states that retained ethnic or colonial connotations in their nomenclature, Valmirica deliberately adopted a constructed, ideologically neutral term that could symbolize an inclusive national identity across racial, ethnic, and regional lines.

Geography

Valmirica, as the world's largest country by land, occupies the heart of northern Edrobe, extending across a vast and diverse natural landscape shaped by millennia of tectonic activity, glacial movements, and fluvial erosion. It is bordered to the southwest by Boravia, to the southeast by Jarnovia, and to the direct south by the icy waters of the Northern Sea, which gives Valmirica a long and rugged coastline defined by dramatic cliffs, deep fjords, and windswept beaches. To the west, the nation borders the mountainous state of Kranmor, while the expansive Drevanian Range forms a natural barrier in the northwest, separating Valmirica from the isolated territory of Noroveld.

The territory of Valmirica is distinguished by its abundance of freshwater, with more rivers and lakes than any other country in the world. The Grand Halver River, which flows from the western highlands near Mount Varell down to the Bay of Riston, forms the backbone of the nation’s hydrological system. It is fed by thousands of tributaries, creeks, and snow-fed streams that carve through forests and lowlands across the continent. The largest inland body of water, Lake Elmond, located in the central region of Vallashire, spans hundreds of kilometers and serves as a defining geographical feature of the interior. Other major lakes include Lorien’s Basin, Lake Meridon, and Traskmere, each surrounded by dense pine forests and steep limestone cliffs.

This massive fluvial network has given rise to enormous expanses of forest, particularly in the northern and central territories. The Tharnwood Wilderness, a temperate rainforest in the northwest, is home to centuries-old trees, granite outcrops, and mist-covered valleys. Further east, the Emerald Wilds stretch across the provinces of Norvaine and East Bravell, forming a near-unbroken canopy of evergreen and deciduous growth. These forests are crisscrossed by clear rivers and dotted with glacial lakes, forming a vibrant green lung that defines the country’s northern identity.

To the south and interior lies the Ravellan Plain, a sweeping grassland region that spans nearly a quarter of the national territory. This plain, shaped by millennia of river sedimentation and glacial retreat, is characterized by flat terrain, rich soils, and wide skies. In spring and summer, the plain erupts into waves of wildflowers, while in autumn it transforms into golden fields stretching from horizon to horizon. The Albarian Steppe, further west, transitions into a more arid and rugged semi-plain, dotted with sandstone formations and seasonal rivers.

In stark contrast to the fertile plains and thick forests of the interior, the southeastern corner of Valmirica is dominated by the Gelmaric Highlands, home to Mount Sareth, the tallest peak in the country. This mountainous region, known locally as the Frostlands of Elgor, is the coldest inhabited region in all of Edrobe, where snowfall is perpetual and temperatures rarely rise above freezing. Glaciers crown the high ridges year-round, and the frozen valleys below are sculpted by ancient ice flows. The city of Elgorath, nestled at the base of the highlands, is one of the most isolated and snowbound urban areas in the world.

Politics

Government

The government of Valmirica operates under a federal presidential republic, grounded in the Constitution, which outlines a rigid separation of powers between the executive, legislative, and judicial branches, while maintaining a robust and highly professional civil service. The state is designed to function under the doctrine of checks and balances, with federal supremacy explicitly enshrined, ensuring uniformity of law across all provinces, territories, and autonomous regions.

At the apex of the executive branch stands the President, who serves as both head of state and head of government, wielding broad authority in domestic administration, foreign relations, and national defense. The President is directly elected through a nationwide popular vote, serving a renewable term fixed by the Constitution. As commander-in-chief of the armed forces, the President holds unilateral control over the deployment of troops, while key appointments to the judiciary, cabinet, and national agencies require legislative confirmation. The executive is assisted by a Vice President, who presides over the upper legislative chamber and assumes presidential powers in cases of incapacity or vacancy.

The executive is further structured through a Cabinet, each heading a core federal department responsible for areas such as finance, defense, foreign affairs, infrastructure, justice, labor, internal security, and environmental stewardship. These departments function alongside dozens of independent federal agencies and commissions, some of which are insulated from direct presidential control, such as the Electoral Commission, the Central Bank, and the Broadcasting Authority. A culture of bureaucratic professionalism is cultivated through a civil service merit system, which is regulated by the Office of Federal Administration, ensuring that appointments and promotions are based on competitive examination and performance.

The bicameral legislature, known collectively as Congress, is composed of the Senate and the House of Representatives. The Senate represents the federated units equally, with two senators per province regardless of population, while the House allocates seats proportionally according to demographic size, updated after each census. Both chambers possess the authority to initiate and amend legislation, approve budgets, declare war, ratify treaties, and conduct investigations into the executive. The House holds exclusive authority over financial legislation, while the Senate confirms appointments and acts as the chamber of impeachment trials. Legislative sessions are held in the Federal Capitol in Montigon, and all sessions are constitutionally required to be open to the public except during national emergencies.

The judiciary operates independently and is tasked with interpreting the law, ensuring constitutional conformity, and resolving disputes between citizens, states, and the federal government. The highest court in the land is the Supreme Court of Valmirica, which exercises final appellate jurisdiction and has the power to strike down federal or state laws deemed unconstitutional. Beneath it lies a structured hierarchy of Federal Circuit Courts, District Courts, and Special Tribunals, including courts dedicated to tax law, military justice, administrative disputes, and national security cases. Judges are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate, with life tenure granted to ensure insulation from political interference.

The civil service in Valmirica is one of the most expansive and institutionalized in the world, encompassing not only the regular departments of government but also a dense network of regulatory agencies, federal commissions, research institutions, and strategic authorities. These bodies implement legislation, oversee compliance, issue binding rulings, and shape national policy in fields ranging from food safety and drug regulation to cybersecurity, space exploration, and nuclear energy. Many agencies possess quasi-legislative and quasi-judicial powers, issuing regulations with the force of law and adjudicating disputes in their domain. The National Administrative Code governs their conduct, ensuring procedural fairness, transparency, and accountability.

Political parties

Valmirica is a constitutional multiparty democracy, but in practice it operates under a de facto two-party system, dominated by the Conservative Party and the Liberal Party, whose rivalry has shaped the country’s political landscape since the ratification of the Constitution. While smaller parties exist and sometimes gain traction at local or regional levels, federal governance has remained in the hands of these two ideological giants, which alternate in power through fiercely contested national elections.

The Conservative Party is the older and more traditionally nationalist of the two, rooted in the foundational values of law, order, sovereignty, and traditionalism. It is strongest in the southern provinces, the farming belt, and the northeastern highlands, where religious conservatism, military patriotism, and economic protectionism remain dominant forces. The party advocates for a strict interpretation of the Constitution, favors federal supremacy over provincial autonomy, and pushes for strong border enforcement, a pro-military foreign policy, and corporate tax cuts. It portrays itself as the guardian of Valmirican values, defending what it calls the “core civilization” against the perceived threats of radical secularism, mass immigration, and cultural relativism. Internally, the party is split between a hardline nationalist wing, known as the Federal Traditionalists, and a more pragmatic Economic Modernist faction, which promotes limited government, deregulation, and free-market capitalism.

Opposing it is the Liberal Party, which represents a coalition of progressives, moderates, labor unions, urban intellectuals, and minority communities. It is most influential in the coastal urban centers, the university towns, and the diverse inland provinces, where multiculturalism, environmentalism, and social justice movements have taken root. The Liberal Party champions civil liberties, universal healthcare, climate legislation, gender equality, and expansive welfare programs. It supports decentralization, often advocating for greater autonomy for provincial governments and reforms to the electoral system to better represent marginalized voices. However, the party is also fractured between a Progressive Left bloc, which pushes for aggressive systemic change, and a Centrist Reformist faction, more focused on economic stability and bipartisan cooperation. Despite these tensions, the Liberals have often managed to build electoral coalitions broad enough to challenge conservative dominance.

While both major parties agree on the federal structure and constitutional permanence, their interpretations of national identity, economic policy, and moral values diverge sharply. The Conservatives appeal to a vision of unity through strength and heritage, while the Liberals emphasize diversity, innovation, and equity. Their alternating rule has resulted in frequent reversals of key policies, especially in areas like environmental regulation, taxation, immigration, and foreign affairs. This dynamic produces a permanent state of political polarization, yet it also prevents either side from entrenching absolute power.

Beyond the two main parties, several minor political formations persist. The Green Bloc occasionally enters parliament with its focus on climate change and social reform, while the Liberty Front, a right-wing populist party, has gained traction in disaffected rural districts. A small but influential National Workers' Union also runs candidates under a laborist banner, particularly in deindustrialized provinces. However, due to the winner-takes-all electoral system, these smaller groups rarely influence federal policymaking except in rare coalition negotiations or runoff elections.

Despite deep divisions and ideological volatility, the party system in Valmirica remains stable, institutionalized, and electorally competitive. Political transitions occur peacefully, election results are accepted by both parties, and democratic norms—though often tested—have endured over successive administrations. What binds the system is a shared belief, however contested in practice, in the Constitution as sacred, and the ballot box as the ultimate arbitrator of power. In this way, Valmirican political life is defined not by consensus, but by perpetual contestation, framed within a rigid constitutional order that tolerates fierce ideological battles so long as they are fought through democratic means.

Administrative subdivisions

Valmirica is organized as a federal union composed of fifteen states, each possessing its own constitution, legislature, executive, and judiciary, yet all bound under the supremacy of the Constitution. The states are co-equal units within the union, possessing broad autonomy in domestic governance but subordinated to federal authority in matters of defense, foreign policy, currency, civil rights, and interstate commerce. Each state sends representatives to the Senate, where all states enjoy equal representation regardless of population, and to the House of Representatives, where representation is proportional.

Every state maintains its own State Governor, elected by popular vote, who leads the State Cabinet, typically composed of secretaries or commissioners responsible for state-level departments such as health, education, policing, infrastructure, and economic development. The State Assembly, often bicameral, enacts state laws, ratifies budgets, and holds oversight powers over the executive. Each state also maintains a State Supreme Court, which interprets state law and serves as the final judicial authority within the state, except in matters involving federal questions.

While all states must respect the fundamental rights guaranteed by the Federal Constitution, they retain the authority to legislate on issues such as education policy, criminal sentencing, taxation, public utilities, land use, and environmental regulation. Several states, particularly in the central and southern regions, have distinct legal traditions rooted in their early post-revolutionary charters, creating a patchwork of legal diversity across the republic. Nonetheless, a strong culture of legal harmonization and intergovernmental coordination ensures that federal and state systems function coherently.

The fifteen states of the United States of Valmirica are: North Arlind, South Arlind, Vallashire, Norvaine, Eldrossa, Bravell, Kessaro, Veltridge, Trenton, Marvale, Elgorath, West Ravellia, East Ravellia, Galdemar, and Halvra.

Foreign relations

As a great power, Valmirica plays a central role in the global order, serving as one of the founding members of the World Union. Valmirica is also a prominent and active member of numerous global institutions, including the Global Reserve Fund, the International Tribunal for Justice, the World Trade Network, the Global Health Organization, and the International Energy Agency. Through its participation in these bodies, Valmirica advances its commitment to economic integration, legal accountability, sustainable development, and collective security, while projecting its identity as the core defender of democracy and human rights across the world.

Valmirica maintains formal diplomatic relations with every recognized sovereign state, adhering to a foreign policy doctrine built on the principles of open engagement, deterrence, and moral leadership. As one of the foundational states of Edrobe, it views itself as the ideological and cultural heart of Edrobean civilization, maintaining cordial and often cooperative ties with all other Edrobean countries. Among these, its special relationship with Comussania stands out as particularly strategic and symbolic. The two countries share deep historical, linguistic, military, and economic ties, and frequently coordinate on major international issues. They are seen by many as the twin pillars of democratic liberalism in Edrobe, despite differences in domestic governance models and social attitudes. Yet Valmirica remains outside the Edrobean Community, preferring instead to preserve sovereign flexibility and global reach, wary of entangling regional obligations that might constrain its ability to act unilaterally on the world stage.

However, Valmirica's global leadership has not been without controversy—particularly across the Global South, where its assertive interventions have earned it a complicated and often hostile reputation. In Tayanusa and Litheria, decades of political interventions, covert operations, and support for regime change have left behind a trail of distrust and political resistance. In countries where Valmirican intelligence agencies allegedly supported coups, imposed sanctions, or pressured governments into alignment, the memory of these actions remains vivid. Despite recent efforts at economic reconciliation and humanitarian diplomacy, Valmirica is still widely viewed as a imperialist force.

Destenia has emerged as the most powerful ideological and geopolitical counterweight to Valmirican influence. As the champion of totalitarian democracy, national sovereignty, and South-South solidarity, Destenia has capitalized on historical resentment toward Valmirica’s past foreign policy to position itself as the voice of the forgotten and the excluded.

Economically, relations between the two have stabilized in recent years, driven by global trade interdependence, mutual investment in digital infrastructure, and joint participation in international frameworks like the World Trade Network. Yet in geopolitical terms, the relationship remains tense, reactive, and unpredictable, marked by competing regional alliances, proxy influence, and sharp divergences in worldview. To champion the global democratic movement, Valmirica funds a vast network of civil society programs, development agencies, and diplomatic missions, targeting nations at risk of backsliding into autocracy or conflict. Through instruments such as the Valmirican Agency for Global Freedom, the Democracy Assistance Fund, and the International Coalition for Open Governance, it actively supports electoral monitoring, independent media, legal reform, and anti-corruption initiatives across Edrobe and beyond.

Military

The United States of Valmirica possesses the second most powerful military force in the world, surpassed only by Destenia, and stands as the premier guarantor of democratic security and strategic deterrence across Edrobe and beyond. Built on the pillars of technological supremacy, global reach, and professional discipline, the Valmirican Armed Forces are structured to operate across all domains—land, sea, air, cyber, and space—with the capacity to project force anywhere on the planet within hours.

The military is composed of these principal branches: the Army, Navy, Air Force. Each branch operates under the authority of the Department of Defense, led by a civilian Secretary appointed by the President and confirmed by the Senate. The President of Valmirica serves as the commander-in-chief, with operational command vested in the Joint Chiefs of Staff, an elite council of top-ranking military officers drawn from each branch. All military actions requiring long-term deployment or declaration of war must receive legislative approval, though the executive maintains the right to act unilaterally in emergencies or in defense of national interests.

  • The Army is the largest branch, trained for both conventional warfare and asymmetric conflict. It is equipped with advanced armored divisions, mechanized infantry, long-range artillery systems, and cutting-edge battlefield robotics. Its elite special forces units, such as the Valmirican Rangers and Task Force Phoenix, are globally deployed for rapid response, counterterrorism, and covert operations.
  • The Navy operates across the globe, with multiple aircraft carrier strike groups, ballistic missile submarines, and an expansive fleet of destroyers, cruisers, and amphibious assault vessels. Its bases on the Southern Coast and in overseas territories grant Valmirica unmatched maritime flexibility, and the Northern Sea Fleet plays a critical role in defending Edrobean waterways.
  • The Air Force commands a fleet of fifth and sixth-generation stealth fighters, hypersonic bombers, aerial refueling systems, and long-range unmanned combat drones capable of precision strikes and real-time surveillance. Through satellite-linked targeting and AI-assisted logistics, Valmirican air power is designed to dominate the skies in any theater of war.

The Valmirican Armed Forces maintain a standing active-duty force of over 1.2 million personnel, supported by a reserve component and the Valmirican National Guard, which may be activated by state governors or federal command in times of emergency. Recruitment is voluntary, though heavily incentivized through state-funded education, healthcare, and pension systems, and all service members undergo rigorous ideological, physical, and academic training through the National Defense Academy System, which includes six elite academies distributed across the states.

Strategically, Valmirica adheres to a doctrine of forward deterrence, maintaining readiness to intervene in any conflict where its core interests or allies are threatened. It conducts joint military exercises with Edrobean democracies, and maintains classified mutual defense arrangements, especially with Comussania and Norvaine. While not formally part of any military alliance system, Valmirica operates a network of strategic partnerships, offering military aid, arms sales, and advisory missions to states aligned with its values. The military-industrial complex within Valmirica is among the most sophisticated on Earth, supported by massive federal investment in aerospace, defense robotics, next-generation weapons, quantum computing, and satellite infrastructure. This synergy between private contractors and public command ensures technological parity with Destenia, even as the two powers diverge in doctrine and ideology.

Law enforcement

Law enforcement and intelligence in the United States of Valmirica are structured as an integrated system of federal, state, and local institutions, designed to uphold the rule of law, protect civil order, and defend the republic from internal and external threats. This system operates under strict constitutional oversight, but with expansive legal authority, granting it the ability to investigate, detain, surveil, and prosecute across the full spectrum of criminal, political, and national security domains.

At the federal level, the backbone of civilian law enforcement is the Valmirican Bureau of Federal Investigation, commonly known as the BFI. Tasked with addressing major federal crimes, public corruption, cybercrime, interstate trafficking, and domestic terrorism, the BFI operates through a nationwide network of field offices and regional fusion centers. Its agents are among the most highly trained operatives in the republic, equipped with the latest forensic, digital, and surveillance tools. The Bureau also oversees joint task forces with state police, and routinely collaborates with foreign counterparts through international legal agreements and intelligence-sharing platforms.

Parallel to the BFI, the Federal Marshals Service handles court security, fugitive apprehension, and witness protection, while the Financial Crimes Directorate targets money laundering, organized crime, and complex white-collar offenses. The Internal Security Division, under the Department of Justice, investigates crimes against state institutions, including espionage, subversion, and paramilitary activity. Its agents, unlike standard law enforcement, operate with classified legal authority, often in close coordination with intelligence services.

Each of the fifteen states maintains its own State Police, which handle law enforcement duties across provincial jurisdictions. These state forces are typically centralized, militarized, and highly autonomous, though subject to federal override in national emergencies or multistate operations. Municipalities maintain local police departments, tasked with community safety, patrol, and enforcement of city ordinances. Despite decentralization, all law enforcement officers are integrated into a unified command network through the National Crime Intelligence Grid, allowing for seamless coordination, tracking, and real-time data access.

Oversight of all law enforcement bodies is conducted by the Office of Civil Protections and Internal Affairs, an independent watchdog with full authority to investigate abuse, corruption, discrimination, or unlawful use of force. Annual reports are submitted to Congress, and all agencies are subject to judicial review, public audit, and legislative inquiry. However, critics argue that in practice, oversight is often weak in high-security contexts, and transparency is frequently curtailed under the pretext of national defense.

The intelligence architecture of Valmirica is among the most sophisticated in the world, directed by the National Intelligence Directorate (NID), a centralized civilian agency responsible for strategic analysis, foreign surveillance, counterintelligence, and cyber warfare. The NID oversees several specialized branches: the Foreign Intelligence Bureau (FIB), the Signals and Electronic Surveillance Agency (SESA), and the National Cyber Command, among others. These agencies are empowered by the Intelligence Operations Act, granting the NID extraordinary powers to monitor communications, infiltrate foreign networks, and disrupt threats before they materialize.

The most secretive component of Valmirica’s intelligence system is the Strategic Operations Command, known informally as "The Shadow Bureau", tasked with black ops, psychological warfare, and foreign regime destabilization. Though officially unacknowledged, its existence is widely assumed by foreign analysts and occasionally referenced in classified budgetary allocations. Operatives within this command are trained in non-conventional warfare, linguistic immersion, and paramilitary engagement, often operating outside the chain of diplomatic communication.

Valmirica’s intelligence services are formally barred from operating domestically, except under emergency provisions or by warrant from a federal tribunal. However, the legal gray zone between foreign and domestic threats has led to growing concern about surveillance overreach, especially in the realm of social media monitoring, encrypted communications interception, and behavioral profiling. Several whistleblower reports in past years have exposed controversial programs targeting activist groups, political dissidents, and journalists, leading to public debate over the balance between security and liberty. In war, crisis, or emergency, all law enforcement and intelligence agencies are placed under the unified operational command of the National Security Council, which can authorize extraordinary domestic powers, including curfews, detention without trial, and suspension of habeas corpus. These emergency powers are rarely invoked, but their existence forms the legal backbone of the state’s capacity to survive existential threats.

Economy

The economy of the United States of Valmirica stands among the largest and most technologically advanced in the world, with a gross domestic product of approximately 4.5 trillion DTR, making it the second-largest economy in Edrobe and a principal driver of the liberal global order. Structured as a federal capitalist system, it combines the dynamism of free enterprise with strong constitutional oversight, fostering an economic culture that prizes innovation, competition, and productivity. The Federal Economic Charter, codified a market-oriented framework emphasizing balanced growth, fiscal prudence, and environmental sustainability, while ensuring that every state and sector contributes to the unity of the national market.

Valmirica’s economy rests on a vast and diversified industrial and technological base. Its traditional heavy industries—steel, shipbuilding, machinery, and refined fuels—remain globally competitive, but the country’s modern strength lies in aerospace engineering, information technology, biotechnology, and advanced robotics. The Department of Commerce and Innovation, working with state governments and private consortia, directs large-scale research and development projects designed to maintain technological independence from rival powers such as Destenia and Cusea. Major industrial clusters extend from the manufacturing corridors of Bravell and West Ravellia to the high-tech innovation belts of Montigon, Vallashire, and Norvaine, where the integration of artificial intelligence and automation has dramatically increased industrial output.

The financial sector, though powerful, is not globally dominant; the Valmirican Dollar (VRD) functions as a strong regional currency, widely used across Edrobe but not a global reserve standard. Its stability is ensured by the Federal Reserve Bank of Valmirica, which regulates monetary supply, interest rates, and capital liquidity within a strictly domestic framework. Financial institutions headquartered in Montigon and Halvra form the backbone of Edrobean banking, financing industrial projects, technological start-ups, and infrastructure modernization. The Montigon Stock Exchange is one of the continent’s largest trading platforms, and the country’s credit rating remains among the highest globally due to disciplined fiscal policy, moderate inflation, and low public debt.

Valmirica’s energy economy is a critical pillar of national self-sufficiency. Rich reserves of coal, natural gas, and uranium support its energy security, but the country has undergone a profound transition toward renewable and low-carbon sources. The Green Liberty Initiative, launched in 2028, has expanded wind, hydro, and solar capacity across the southern plains and coastal regions, positioning Valmirica as an Edrobean leader in environmental technology. Government subsidies and private investment have spurred the growth of green industries, transforming the nation’s energy infrastructure and creating millions of new jobs in clean engineering, battery storage, and sustainable manufacturing.

The agricultural sector, centered in South Arlind, Marvale, and the Ravellan Plain, remains one of the most productive on the continent. Fertile soils, mechanized farming, and biogenetic crop innovation allow Valmirica to maintain agricultural self-sufficiency and export surplus food to neighboring states. The National Agricultural Board coordinates subsidies, market stabilization, and emergency reserves, ensuring food security even under climate stress. The agrarian heartland is also home to powerful cooperatives and farm unions that influence both local governance and national economic planning.

The services and digital economy constitute over two-thirds of GDP, driven by finance, software, telecommunications, education, healthcare, and cultural industries. Valmirican universities and research institutes attract talent from across Edrobe, producing a highly skilled workforce that sustains innovation in biotechnology, cybersecurity, and applied physics. The creative industries—film, design, and media—are exported worldwide, reinforcing the country’s cultural soft power. Tourism also contributes significantly to the economy, with millions visiting the historic cities of Montigon, Bravell, and Kessaro, as well as the glacial landscapes of Elgorath and the forested wilderness of Norvaine.

Infrastructure in Valmirica is a monument to engineering modernity. The Federal Infrastructure Authority maintains a dense network of high-speed rail lines, smart highways, and deepwater ports that integrate the continental interior with the northern coast. The national energy grid is fully digitalized, and the 8G communication framework ensures uninterrupted connectivity across the federation. Automation and AI-based logistics have transformed urban mobility, while the Aerial Freight Network, a system of autonomous cargo drones, connects regional markets with unprecedented speed.

Internationally, Valmirica is a trade superpower, though it carefully balances openness with protection of strategic sectors. Its principal exports include aerospace technology, industrial machinery, electronics, vehicles, pharmaceuticals, and defense systems, while imports consist mainly of rare minerals, tropical goods, and consumer materials. It maintains a strong trade partnership with Comussania, forming the largest liberal democratic economic corridor in Edrobe, while relations with Destenia remain tense yet economically unavoidable due to shared digital infrastructure and maritime trade routes. Valmirica advocates for fair trade and intellectual property protections through the World Trade Network but resists any supranational authority that could override domestic legislation, preserving its sovereignty in economic decision-making.

Socially, the Valmirican economic model combines market liberalism with social responsibility. Federal law guarantees universal access to healthcare, education, and social insurance, financed through progressive taxation and corporate contributions. The Labor and Employment Code ensures collective bargaining, safe working conditions, and gender equality in the workforce. Public pensions and unemployment benefits form a strong welfare foundation, while incentives for entrepreneurship and small business innovation maintain economic mobility. Income inequality remains a persistent challenge, yet nationwide housing, education, and digital access programs have significantly reduced regional disparities.

In the wider Edrobean and global context, the Valmirican economy symbolizes the synthesis of freedom, productivity, and moral restraint. It is not the financial center of the world, nor the issuer of a universal reserve currency, but rather the industrial and technological heart of the democratic world order. Its wealth is grounded not in speculation, but in invention; its influence not in coercion, but in example. Demography

The demography of the United States of Valmirica reflects the profile of a mature, industrially advanced, and highly urbanized society, shaped by decades of population planning, social welfare, and educational expansion. With a population exceeding 121 million inhabitants, Valmirica ranks among the most populous nations of Edrobe, serving as both a demographic and cultural giant in the northern hemisphere. The median age stands at 39.8 years, indicative of a balanced demographic structure in which youth vitality coexists with the stability of an aging but economically active middle generation. Sustained birth rates, combined with selective immigration from neighboring Edrobean countries, have preserved demographic equilibrium and prevented the sharp population decline experienced by many developed states.

Over 82 percent of Valmiricans live in urban areas, reflecting the country’s transformation into a thoroughly metropolitan civilization. The major urban corridors—Montigon–Vallashire, Ravellia–Bravell, and Norvaine–Halvra—form interconnected megaregions that anchor the national economy. These cities are characterized by dense public transit networks, mixed-use development, and high environmental standards under the Federal Urban Renewal Program, which promotes sustainable housing, waste management, and energy-efficient construction. Rural areas, while sparsely populated, remain economically integrated through digital infrastructure and agricultural modernization, ensuring that even remote communities enjoy access to essential public services.

The education system of Valmirica is one of the most comprehensive in the world, reflecting the national ideal that knowledge is the foundation of liberty. Public education is compulsory and fully state-funded through secondary level, while tertiary education enjoys a mixed system of public and private institutions under strict federal accreditation. Literacy rates approach 99.7 percent, and over 70 percent of young adults pursue higher education, particularly in fields related to science, technology, engineering, and public administration. The National University Network, composed of more than two hundred colleges and research institutes, produces a globally competitive workforce that fuels the country’s innovation-driven economy. Government investment in lifelong learning and vocational training ensures continuous upskilling, reducing structural unemployment and preserving social mobility across all social classes.

In terms of gender equality, Valmirica is widely regarded as one of the most progressive societies in Edrobe. Women constitute nearly 49 percent of the labor force, and gender parity has been achieved in public education, healthcare, and civil service representation. The Equal Status Act guarantees full legal equality in pay, political participation, and property ownership, while the Federal Commission on Social Inclusion monitors institutional compliance with gender balance targets. The rise of women in leadership roles—both in government and in major industries—has become emblematic of the Valmirican belief that liberty must be measured not only in rights but in opportunity.

The Valmirican Human Development Index (VHDI) ranks among the top five globally, consistently exceeding 0.940, reflecting extraordinary standards of life expectancy, education, and income. Universal healthcare ensures an average life expectancy of 83.2 years, while comprehensive social welfare, accessible housing, and environmental protection contribute to high living standards across the federation. Regional disparities between northern and southern provinces have been largely mitigated by the Equitable Growth Act, which redistributes federal investment toward underdeveloped areas, fostering inclusive development and regional cohesion.

Migration within Valmirica remains fluid and internally balanced. Large-scale rural-to-urban migration, once dominant in the early decades after unification, has given way to cyclical migration patterns driven by education, professional advancement, and lifestyle preference. Foreign immigration is moderate and regulated through a merit-based system prioritizing skilled professionals, researchers, and refugees from allied nations. Integration programs emphasize linguistic and civic education, reinforcing national cohesion without suppressing cultural diversity.

Demographically, Valmirica represents a society at the apex of human development, blending stability with adaptability. Its citizens enjoy high life expectancy, universal literacy, and broad access to opportunity, all under a system that prizes equality, merit, and social dignity. The result is a nation whose demographic vitality mirrors its political ideal: a republic built on strength through liberty, and liberty sustained through knowledge and human advancement.

Language

The official language of the United States is Valmirican, a modern standardized tongue descended from the old Edrobean linguistic family, refined through centuries of cultural and administrative consolidation. It serves as the sole language of government, education, and public administration, symbolizing unity across the federation’s fifteen states. Valmirican is characterized by a precise grammar, rich vocabulary, and a flexible syntax that allows for both formal and expressive discourse, reflecting the nation’s values of clarity and civic reason. While regional accents and dialectal variations exist—particularly in Norvaine, Bravell, and the Ravellian plain—the written and broadcast standard remains uniform, enforced by the National Language Council to maintain linguistic cohesion.

Religion

The religion of the United States of Valmirica is defined by a tradition of constitutional secularism combined with a deep cultural heritage rooted in Christian humanism. Approximately 82 percent of Valmiricans identify as Christians, predominantly adhering to the Reformed Valmirican Church, a broad and decentralized denomination that emphasizes personal faith, moral discipline, and civic duty rather than clerical hierarchy. The Church plays a cultural rather than political role, serving as a moral foundation for communities while remaining separate from the affairs of state.

Around 15 percent of the population identifies as irreligious, including atheists, agnostics, and secular humanists, reflecting the nation’s intellectual pluralism and freedom of conscience. A smaller Muslim minority of about 3 percent, concentrated mainly in the southern provinces, practices its faith freely under constitutional protection. Religious freedom is guaranteed by the Charter of Civil Rights, which prohibits discrimination on the basis of belief and ensures equal access to education, employment, and public service for all citizens.

Public life in Valmirica is thus secular in governance but moral in character, guided by a civic ethic that views faith as a private virtue and not a political instrument. The result is a society where spiritual diversity coexists with national unity, and where belief—whether religious or secular—serves the shared ideal of moral strength through liberty.

Ethnicities

The ethnic composition of the United States of Valmirica is predominantly Valmirican, accounting for roughly 87 percent of the population, forming the historical, linguistic, and cultural core of the nation. The Valmirican people trace their origins to the early Edrobean settlers and revolutionary communities that united during the War of Independence, forging a collective identity centered on liberty, civic virtue, and national strength. This shared heritage—rooted in common language, education, and constitutional tradition—remains the foundation of social cohesion and national consciousness.

The remaining 13 percent of the population consists of immigrants and their descendants, primarily originating from other Edrobean countries such as Comussania, Boravia, Jarnovia, and Agaritia, as well as from Tayanusan nations including Cusea, Lamberia, and Ricene. These communities are concentrated in major urban centers like Montigon, Bravell, and Norvaine, where they contribute significantly to the industrial, academic, and cultural life of the republic. Many have fully integrated into Valmirican society through the civic naturalization system, which emphasizes allegiance to the Constitution, proficiency in the Valmirican language, and participation in public service.

Although Valmirica maintains a strong civic identity rather than an ethnic one, it recognizes the cultural value brought by these communities in enriching its social fabric. Festivals, cuisine, and artistic expression from the wider Edrobean and Tayanusan worlds have blended into Valmirican life, symbolizing a patriotic inclusiveness that balances cultural diversity with national unity. Thus, Valmirican ethnicity is understood not merely as descent, but as citizenship through shared principles—the belief that belonging is defined by loyalty to liberty, law, and the republic itself.
