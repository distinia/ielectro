Cavallesia, officially the Republic of Cavallesia, is a sovereign country located in southeastern Edrobe. It is an archipelago nation composed of two main islands—Cavallesian Island and Velkstra Island—alongside ten smaller islets, situated between the Grandic Ocean to the east and the Gulf of Agaritia to the west. It shares maritime borders with Agaritia and Comussania to the north, Destenia to the west, and the open ocean to the east.

With a population of approximately 1.25 million, Cavallesia is one of the smallest nations in the world by size and population, yet it maintains a high level of development and a strong international presence. Its capital and largest city is Drijet. The country operates under a unitary presidential republic, with the President serving as both head of state and government, supported by a unicameral National Assembly and a Supreme Court.

Cavallesia’s official language is Cavallesian. The population is 95% ethnically Cavallesian, predominantly Christian (Protestant), and enjoys high levels of literacy, healthcare access, and gender equality.

Economically, Cavallesia ranks 15th globally in GDP, driven by a service-oriented economy with strong sectors in finance, maritime trade, innovation, and tourism. Despite its size, the country plays a strategic role in regional logistics due to its advanced port infrastructure and central position in southern Edrobe.

Cavallesia is a full member of several major international and regional organizations, including the United Nations, Edrobean Community, Global Reserve Fund, International Tribunal for Justice, World Trade Network, Global Health Organization, and the International Energy Agency. Known for its neutral foreign policy, efficient governance, and high human development index, Cavallesia is widely considered a model of small-state success.

Etymology

The name Cavallesia originates from the Cavallesian word “Kaaval,” which translates to “Horse.” This etymology is closely tied to both the geography and cultural heritage of the nation. The largest island in the archipelago—Cavallesian Island—is notably shaped like a horse when viewed from above, a characteristic that left a lasting impression on early settlers and cartographers.

However, the symbolism of the horse in Cavallesia goes far beyond geographical coincidence. Despite the nation’s mountainous terrain and lack of rivers, horses have historically played a vital role in transportation, agricultural labor, and rural mobility. In the more isolated and rugged inland regions, pastoral communities have relied on horses not only as working animals but as an integral part of daily life, economy, and tradition.

Over time, the horse became a national symbol of resilience, strength, and independence, traits deeply valued in Cavallesian society. It is often depicted in folklore, art, and even state insignia, reflecting the enduring connection between the people and their environment.

Geography

Cavallesia is a compact archipelagic nation located in southeastern Edrobe, composed of two main islands—Cavallesian Island and Velkstra Island—alongside ten smaller islets scattered throughout its maritime territory. The country lies between the Grandic Ocean to the east and the Gulf of Agaritia to the west, giving it strategic access to major regional trade routes and naval corridors.

Its maritime borders are shared with several key Edrobean nations: to the north, it borders Agaritia and Comussania, while to the west it is adjacent to Destenia, and to the east it opens directly into the vast expanse of the Grandic Ocean. This positioning has made Cavallesia both a gateway to eastern Edrobe and a valuable hub for maritime commerce and diplomacy.

Geographically, Cavallesia is predominantly mountainous, with rugged terrain, steep coastal cliffs, and limited flatlands. The mountainous landscape shapes not only the country’s scenic beauty but also its infrastructure, settlement patterns, and economic activities. There are no major rivers in Cavallesia, making the country reliant on rainwater collection, groundwater reserves, and desalination systems for its water needs.

Forests cover much of the higher elevations, while terraced agriculture and coastal towns define the more habitable zones. The natural geography has also encouraged environmental preservation, and many areas remain ecologically protected due to their biodiversity and cultural significance.

Politics

Government

Cavallesia is a unitary presidential republic, where the structure of government is organized around the principle of centralized executive authority, balanced by legislative and judicial independence. At the core of the executive branch is the President of the Republic, who serves as both head of state and head of government, embodying national unity and overseeing the effective administration of the country. The President is elected directly by the citizens in a national election held every six years, with no intermediary bodies involved in the selection process. This direct mandate grants the President a powerful democratic legitimacy.

The President appoints and leads the Cabinet, which is composed of various Ministers responsible for specific sectors such as defense, foreign affairs, finance, education, and health. These Ministers are chosen solely at the discretion of the President and are accountable to him. The Cabinet functions as the highest executive decision-making body, formulating policies, proposing legislation, and managing the operations of the national bureaucracy. Alongside the Cabinet, a wide array of state agencies, commissions, and regulatory bodies ensure that government services are delivered efficiently and in accordance with established law.

The legislative power in Cavallesia is exercised by the National Assembly, a unicameral legislature consisting of 100 representatives elected by the people for a term of five years. The National Assembly has the authority to enact legislation, approve the national budget, ratify international treaties, and exercise oversight over the actions of the executive branch. Debates in the Assembly are guided by democratic principles, and representatives are expected to act in the interest of their constituents while respecting the integrity of the constitutional order.

Judicial authority is vested in the Supreme Court of Cavallesia, the apex institution of the national judiciary. The Court is headed by the Chief Justice, who presides over its operations and ensures the independence and integrity of judicial proceedings. The Supreme Court is responsible for interpreting the Constitution, resolving legal disputes between branches of government, reviewing the legality of executive actions, and adjudicating appeals from lower courts. Most importantly, it acts as the guardian of constitutional order, protecting the rule of law, civil liberties, and fundamental rights of all Cavallesian citizens. Through its decisions, the Court plays a vital role in maintaining checks and balances within the government and upholding the democratic character of the republic.

Political parties

Cavallesia’s political landscape is defined by the presence of three main parties that have alternated in government through various coalition arrangements since the country’s democratic consolidation. The National Party remains the largest and most influential, historically serving as the backbone of Cavallesian governance. Its platform emphasizes national unity, economic modernization, and a strong centralized state, promoting stability and continuity in both domestic and foreign affairs.

Alongside it, the Progressive Alliance represents the reformist and social-liberal wing of Cavallesian politics, advocating for civil liberties, environmental protection, and regional development, often appealing to the younger and urban electorate. The People’s Conservative Union, on the other hand, embodies the traditionalist and nationalist segment of society, focusing on sovereignty, cultural preservation, and defense policy. These three forces frequently form coalition governments, with the National Party typically acting as the senior partner in most administrations.

Despite periods of rivalry, cooperation among these major parties has been essential to maintaining political stability and institutional continuity in Cavallesia’s parliamentary system. Smaller parties—ranging from regionalists to greens and libertarians—occasionally enter alliances but rarely achieve significant legislative presence. The nation’s democracy remains formally pluralistic, with free elections, legal opposition, and constitutional guarantees of expression and association, though some observers note that coalition politics and entrenched party interests have at times constrained deeper political renewal and grassroots participation.

Administrative subdivisions

Cavallesia is administratively divided into 12 provinces, each corresponding to one of the country’s islands. Given Cavallesia’s small geographic size and relatively low population, the structure of governance is highly centralized, with most key decisions and policies directed from the national capital, Drijet. This centralization allows for efficient coordination, streamlined policy implementation, and a uniform standard of governance across the entire archipelago.

Despite this central control, each province retains a degree of local administrative authority, primarily focused on the management of public services, infrastructure, and community affairs. Provinces are headed by Commissioners, who are appointed directly by the central government and serve as its official representatives at the provincial level. These Commissioners are responsible for coordinating state activities, overseeing provincial budgets, and ensuring that national laws and policies are properly enforced within their jurisdictions.

Below the provincial level, Cavallesia is further subdivided into municipalities, which serve as the basic units of local self-governance. Each municipality is led by a Mayor, who is tasked with managing the day-to-day affairs of the town or city, including urban planning, public utilities, education, and cultural activities. While Mayors operate with a certain degree of autonomy in local matters, they remain legally accountable to the provincial authorities and ultimately the central government.

This administrative model reflects Cavallesia’s emphasis on efficiency, national cohesion, and bureaucratic discipline, allowing the country to maintain effective governance while accommodating the unique characteristics of its insular geography.

Foreign relations

Cavallesia maintains an active and principled approach to foreign relations, shaped by its geographic position in southeastern Edrobe, its status as a small but stable democracy, and its commitment to multilateral cooperation. Despite its limited size and resources, Cavallesia has established itself as a respected diplomatic actor, known for its neutral stance in major conflicts, promotion of international law, and support for collective action through global institutions.

The country is a proud member of several key international and regional organizations, including the United Nations, Edrobean Community, Global Reserve Fund, World Trade Network, International Tribunal for Justice, International Energy Agency, and the Global Health Organization. Through these platforms, Cavallesia contributes to peacekeeping efforts, environmental sustainability, public health cooperation, and economic development, advocating especially for the needs and interests of small nations within the global system.

Within the Edrobean Community, Cavallesia plays an active role in shaping common policy, particularly in the areas of justice, social policy, and human rights, where its legal and civic institutions are often cited as models of integrity. While it lacks the military weight or economic influence of larger members like Destenia or Comussania, Cavallesia often acts as a mediator in intra-community disputes, leveraging its reputation for political neutrality and institutional stability.

Cavallesia maintains cordial bilateral relations with all neighboring states, especially Laocitia, Alveria, and Destenia, with whom it shares strong trade and transport links. It also supports deeper ties with non-Edrobean partners, like Valmirica and Fesia, especially through cultural exchange, technical assistance programs, and sustainable development initiatives.

A cornerstone of Cavallesian diplomacy is its commitment to sovereignty, peaceful coexistence, and non-interference. The country opposes hegemonic influence and actively promotes the idea that even the smallest states must have an equal voice in international decision-making. In an increasingly polarized world, Cavallesia continues to position itself as a bridge-builder, fostering dialogue, promoting multilateralism, and championing the values of unity and progress on the world stage.

Military

The Armed Forces of Cavallesia are a professional, technologically advanced, and strategically focused military institution tasked with ensuring the sovereignty, defense, and maritime security of the Republic of Cavallesia. Despite the country’s limited size and peaceful foreign policy, the Cavallesian military is highly respected for its efficiency, discipline, and integration with civilian emergency and infrastructure systems. It operates under the direct authority of the President, who serves as Commander-in-Chief, with oversight from the National Security Council and the Ministry of Defense.

Cavallesia maintains a defensive military doctrine, emphasizing deterrence, rapid response, and territorial integrity. It does not pursue power projection or overseas military bases, in line with its constitutionally enshrined policy of permanent neutrality. The Cavallesian military is divided into three main branches: the Cavallesian Army, the Cavallesian Air Force, the Cavallesian Navy. Each branch operates independently but is coordinated through a unified command structure headquartered in Drijet.

  • The Cavallesian Army serves as the country’s ground defense and territorial security force. It is composed of light infantry brigades trained for rapid deployment, mountain warfare, and civil support operations. Given Cavallesia’s insular geography, the CDF maintains a modest number of armored vehicles, relying instead on mobility, surveillance, and defensive positioning in fortified coastal zones and elevated outposts.
  • The Cavallesian Air Force serves as the aerial defense and rapid-response branch, ensuring national airspace security, reconnaissance, and support to both naval and ground operations. Despite the country’s compact size, the CAF is a technologically advanced and highly specialized component, emphasizing mobility, precision, and multi-terrain adaptability. Its pilots undergo rigorous high-altitude and mountain-aerobatic training over Cavallesia’s rugged interior ranges, mastering flight under extreme wind and visibility conditions. The Air Force operates a mixed fleet of light multirole fighters, transport aircraft, and surveillance drones, providing both defensive interception and logistical capabilities.
  • The Cavallesian Navy is the largest and most active branch, responsible for naval defense, coastal surveillance, maritime rescue, and anti-smuggling operations. It operates a fleet of fast-attack boats, patrol ships, and coastal defense drones, with its main naval base located in the port of Elsenaar on Cavallesian Island. The CMG plays a critical role in maintaining security along Cavallesia’s trade routes and enforcing environmental protection laws in the country’s Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ).

Military service in Cavallesia is voluntary, with no conscription in place. However, the country maintains a national readiness program, requiring all citizens aged 18 to 25 to undergo a one-week civil defense seminar focused on first aid, evacuation procedures, and national emergency protocols. Veterans enjoy high social standing, with preferential access to public employment, education benefits, and medical care.

The military budget of Cavallesia is modest, accounting for approximately 1.4% of national GDP, which reflects its defensive posture and reliance on strategic deterrence over force projection. Spending is directed primarily toward cybersecurity, naval modernization, coastal fortification, and surveillance systems. The armed forces make extensive use of artificial intelligence, encrypted communications, autonomous maritime sensors, and geospatial tracking to ensure real-time awareness and precision in decision-making. Cavallesia does not manufacture weapons domestically on a large scale, but it maintains a tightly regulated defense procurement system. All arms acquisitions are subject to parliamentary approval and strict ethical review, with an emphasis on sustainability and non-lethality wherever possible.

Law enforcement

The Law Enforcement of Cavallesia is a centralized, professional, and strictly regulated apparatus composed primarily of the National Police Directorate (NPD) and the Cavallesian Intelligence Bureau (CIB). These institutions function under the oversight of the Ministry of Internal Affairs, reporting directly to the President and accountable to the National Assembly through annual transparency audits and legal review.

The National Police Directorate serves as the primary law enforcement body throughout Cavallesia. It is a unified and civilian-led force with national jurisdiction, responsible for public safety, criminal investigation, traffic regulation, counter-narcotics, and the enforcement of civil law. The NPD is a single, non-militarized entity, ensuring legal uniformity and operational efficiency across the country’s twelve provinces and all municipalities. Its leadership is held by the Chief Commissioner of Police, a presidential appointee with a fixed renewable term of six years. The National Police is organized into several specialized divisions, including the Criminal Investigations Division, Public Order and Riot Control Division, Cybercrime Unit, Narcotics Control Bureau, Forensics Department, and the Community Policing Division. This last division focuses on neighborhood patrols, citizen engagement, and de-escalation training, which are key pillars of Cavallesia’s preventative policing strategy.

Police officers are required to complete rigorous academic and field training at the National Police Academy in Drijet, followed by continuous professional development courses throughout their careers. The selection process is merit-based and includes psychological testing, ethical examinations, and extensive background checks. Use of force is tightly regulated, with non-lethal measures preferred and independently investigated in cases of misconduct. Body cameras are mandated for all operational units, and police-citizen interactions are regularly reviewed by the Independent Police Ethics Commission, a civilian watchdog body. Cavallesia maintains low crime rates, thanks to proactive social policies, high urban surveillance, and strong institutional trust. The public perception of law enforcement is generally positive, supported by transparency, accountability, and a responsive complaint system.

The Cavallesian Intelligence Bureau is the national intelligence agency, responsible for both domestic and foreign intelligence gathering, counterintelligence, counterterrorism, and the protection of state secrets and digital infrastructure. The CIB operates independently from the police, with its own chain of command and internal protocols. It is headed by the Director of National Intelligence, who is appointed by the President and confirmed by the National Assembly in closed session.

The CIB’s operations focus heavily on cybersecurity, foreign espionage threats, organized crime networks, and ideological extremism, with specific priority given to threats that may undermine national unity, public order, or Cavallesia’s sovereign institutions. Intelligence officers are bound by a strict legal framework that prohibits political spying, media surveillance, or unauthorized data collection. All covert operations must be authorized by a classified oversight committee composed of Assembly members, judges, and defense officials.

The Bureau is equipped with state-of-the-art digital tools, artificial intelligence systems, and encrypted communication networks, and it works closely with academic institutions and private sector experts to anticipate and neutralize evolving threats. Despite its advanced capabilities, the CIB adheres to a principle of strategic minimalism, avoiding intrusive domestic surveillance and focusing on precision intelligence. Both the NPD and CIB collaborate regularly with the Judiciary, ensuring that all detentions, investigations, and surveillance activities comply with constitutional rights and international human rights standards. Warrants must be approved by a judicial officer, and all interrogations are recorded and legally monitored. Cavallesia has no paramilitary police or overlapping security forces. All coercive authority is centralized and highly institutionalized to prevent abuse of power, corruption, or duplication of roles. The integration between the intelligence services and the police remains limited and regulated, preserving civilian supremacy and legal independence.

Economy

Cavallesia boasts a highly developed and diversified economy, ranking 15th globally with a GDP of 420 billion DTR. Despite its modest territorial size and limited natural resources, the country has built a resilient, export-oriented economy by leveraging its strategic maritime location, high human capital, and transparent institutions.

The economy is primarily service-based, with strong sectors including finance, maritime logistics, education, digital technologies, and tourism. The capital, Drijet, serves as a major financial and commercial center, known for its efficient regulatory environment, stable currency, and low corruption. Cavallesia attracts regional and international investment due to its open markets and business-friendly governance.

Cavallesia's geographic position between the Grandic Ocean and the Gulf of Agaritia makes it a key logistics hub. Its advanced port infrastructure and competitive shipping services make it one of the top transshipment centers for container traffic in the hemisphere. Maritime trade forms a vital part of national revenue, supported by modern customs systems and free-trade zones.

The tourism sector has expanded rapidly, drawing visitors to the country’s scenic mountains, island landscapes, and cultural sites. A strong emphasis on sustainable tourism has helped balance environmental preservation with economic growth, generating employment and foreign currency while safeguarding biodiversity.

Although Cavallesia’s agriculture and fisheries are constrained by its terrain, the country has invested in high-efficiency, tech-driven practices to ensure food security. Small-scale terraced farming, aquaculture, and offshore fishing supply domestic markets and support rural livelihoods.

A standout feature of Cavallesia’s economy is its focus on education, innovation, and clean technologies. The state invests heavily in digital infrastructure, research, and green energy, positioning itself as a global leader in sustainable development and technological adaptation. Public-private partnerships fuel progress in areas like renewable energy, biotech, and cybersecurity.

Cavallesia participates actively in the global economy through its membership in the World Trade Network, Global Reserve Fund, and International Energy Agency, and benefits from extensive trade agreements. Its currency, the Cavallesian Rand (CVR), is stable, convertible, and widely respected in international markets.

With low unemployment, a comprehensive welfare system, and fiscal discipline, Cavallesia has achieved a rare balance of economic growth and social inclusion. Its model of smart governance, strategic openness, and human-centered development has made it one of the most successful small economies in the world.

Demography

Cavallesia has a population of approximately 1,245,789 people, making it one of the smallest countries in Edrobe by population, yet one of the most socially advanced. Despite its limited size, Cavallesia enjoys high standards in education, healthcare, and gender equality, reflecting a society that prioritizes human development and institutional efficiency.

The median age in Cavallesia is 37 years, indicating a balanced population structure with a healthy mix of youth, working-age adults, and retirees. The demographic trend is marked by low birth rates, consistent with urbanized and developed societies, but offset by high life expectancy and strong public health systems, which maintain a stable and sustainable population level.

Gender equality is a core value in Cavallesian society and is strongly reflected in both legal frameworks and social norms. Women and men have equal access to education, employment, and political participation. The government enforces strict anti-discrimination laws and actively promotes female leadership in both the public and private sectors. Gender representation in institutions—especially in education, healthcare, and local governance—is remarkably balanced.

Cavallesia’s education system is publicly funded and universally accessible, with near-total literacy rates and high enrollment across all levels. From early childhood through to higher education, the curriculum emphasizes civic education, science, and multilingualism, ensuring that students are well-prepared for both national service and international engagement. Universities and technical institutes are concentrated in the capital, Drijet, with satellite campuses and distance learning options available across the archipelago.

In terms of public health, Cavallesia maintains a universal healthcare system that provides comprehensive services to all citizens, funded through a national insurance model. Preventive care, vaccination programs, and health education are widespread, contributing to low infant mortality, high life expectancy, and effective management of chronic and infectious diseases. The healthcare workforce is well-trained and digitally integrated, allowing for efficient service delivery even in remote island communities.

Language

The official language of Cavallesia is Cavallesian, a unique language belonging to the Edrobean linguistic family, with strong historical and structural ties to Comussan and Agaritian. It is spoken natively by the vast majority of the population and serves as the sole language of government, education, judiciary, and media. As a unifying cultural cornerstone, Cavallesian reinforces a strong sense of national identity and civic cohesion across the entire archipelago.

Given the country's highly homogeneous population and low levels of immigration, linguistic uniformity is widespread. Cavallesian is taught exclusively in schools and used across all institutions, with no significant presence of minority or foreign languages. The government places a high emphasis on linguistic preservation and promotion, supporting language academies, publishing initiatives, and public media content to ensure that Cavallesian continues to thrive in both formal and informal settings.

Religion

Religion plays a central cultural role in Cavallesia, where 95% of the population identifies as Christian, making it one of the most religiously homogeneous countries in Edrobe. Christianity in Cavallesia is predominantly Protestant, shaped by centuries of religious tradition, civic morality, and national customs. Churches are deeply embedded in the fabric of community life, not only as places of worship but also as centers for education, social services, and cultural gatherings.

The relationship between religion and the state is defined by a model of pragmatic secularism. While the government does not endorse any specific denomination, it maintains a respectful cooperation with religious institutions, especially in areas like charity work, public ethics, and national ceremonies. Religious holidays are official public holidays, and it is common for national events to begin with ecumenical prayers or blessings. However, the legal and educational systems remain officially secular, and religious freedom is constitutionally guaranteed.

The remaining 5% of the population adheres to other beliefs or identifies as non-religious. Minority religions exist in small numbers, primarily among diplomatic families or isolated immigrant communities, and while their presence is limited, they are allowed to practice freely. Religious discrimination is rare, and tolerance is a core social value, upheld by both law and tradition.

Overall, religion in Cavallesia is a source of national cohesion, shaping values such as discipline, compassion, and social responsibility. Although modernity and urbanization have led to some generational shifts in religious practice, faith remains a defining pillar of Cavallesian identity and public life.

Ethnicities

Cavallesia is one of the most ethnically homogeneous nations in Edrobe, with 95% of the population identifying as Cavallesian. This dominant ethnic group has shaped the country’s language, traditions, social norms, and national identity. The Cavallesians are culturally and linguistically distinct from most other Edrobean peoples, sharing closer historical ties with the Comussans and Agaritians, particularly in linguistic heritage and early settlement patterns.

The remaining 5% of the population consists of small ethnic minorities, primarily from neighboring Edrobean countries such as Laocitia, Metosia, and Verdania, as well as a limited number of Tayanusan migrants. These communities are concentrated mostly in urban areas and port cities, particularly in Drijet, where they contribute to commerce, diplomacy, and certain technical sectors.

Cavallesia promotes a strong policy of national integration rooted in civic identity rather than ethnicity. While the state recognizes the presence of minorities, it emphasizes unity through shared language, law, and citizenship, rather than multiculturalism. All citizens, regardless of origin, are expected to participate fully in Cavallesian society through the adoption of Cavallesian language and values.

Thanks to this integration model and the small size of minority populations, ethnic tensions are virtually non-existent, and the country enjoys a high degree of social cohesion and stability. The homogeneous nature of the population also supports the state’s centralized governance model and simplifies the administration of public services, education, and national defense.
