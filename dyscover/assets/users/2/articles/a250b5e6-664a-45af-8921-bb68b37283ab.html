Verdania, officially the Kingdom of Verdania, is a country located in the northeastern region of Edrobe, bordered by Jarnovia to the north, Comussania to the east, Agaritia to the south, and the Northern Sea to the west. With an area of 42,365 km² and a population of 5.3 million, Verdania is renowned for its homogeneity, stability, and cultural continuity. Its landscape features the Hoefen and Wester Forests, the Manchel River, and the Chisshen Archipelago, emphasizing a natural heritage deeply respected by its people.

The country is governed by a parliamentary democracy under a hereditary monarchy, with King Jakob II as head of state and Prime Minister Vilhelm Brun as head of government. The unicameral Parliament, holds legislative authority, while Supreme Judge Oliver Taylor oversees the independent judiciary. Verdania is administratively divided into five counties, including the Capital County of Uzdonstad, with governance centralized but services delivered efficiently at regional and municipal levels.

Politically, Verdania is a model of democratic moderation and stability, with a competitive multi-party system led by the Royal Conservative Party, the Social Democratic Union, and the Centrist People’s Alliance. All elections are free, fair, and transparent, with high civic participation and strong institutional trust. The country’s law enforcement is centralized, disciplined, and guided by constitutional norms, supported by a well-regulated justice system and internal accountability mechanisms.

Economically, Verdania ranks 13th globally, with a GDP of 440 billion DTR. It boasts a diversified economy driven by precision manufacturing, digital services, and renewable energy, supplemented by sustainable agriculture and strong trade ties, especially with Destenia and Agaritia. The kingdom enjoys low public debt, high employment, and strong regulatory institutions, reflecting its values of fiscal prudence and social equity.

Religiously, Verdania is overwhelmingly Catholic (95%), with the Catholic Church playing a central cultural and charitable role. Though the state is secular, the Church maintains close symbolic ties with the monarchy and retains influence in education, healthcare, and public life. Other religious groups exist but remain minimal and fully protected under constitutional law.

Verdania’s foreign policy is rooted in multilateralism, diplomacy, and regional cooperation. It is a founding member of the United Nations and the Edrobean Community, and it has strong bilateral ties with Jarnovia, Comussania, Destenia, and Agaritia. The kingdom promotes peaceful coexistence, economic integration, and sovereign neutrality, balancing traditional values with strategic engagement in a complex geopolitical environment.

Etymology

The name Verdania derives from the ancient Edrobean root "verd", meaning "green" or "fertile land", a reference to the region’s historically lush plains and forested hills that once formed the core of early Verdanian settlements. Over time, the term evolved into Verdania, symbolizing “the land of green” or “the green realm,” a name that came to reflect not only the physical environment but also the kingdom’s pastoral identity, agricultural tradition, and divine association with natural abundance.

The suffix -ia, common in Edrobean place names, was formally adopted with the establishment of the monarchy to denote territorial unity and sovereign status. The earliest recorded use of the name Verdania in an official context appears in royal charters from the early 12th century, during the consolidation of regional duchies under a single crown. As the kingdom developed, the name acquired further symbolic weight, becoming associated with stability, tradition, and divine protection, especially under the royal ideology expressed through the national motto: “God shall defend us.”

Geography

Verdania is located in the northeastern region of the Edrobean continent, bordered to the north by Jarnovia, to the east by Comussania, to the south by Agaritia, and to the west by the vast Northern Sea. Despite its relatively compact area of 42,365 square kilometers, the country possesses a diverse and ecologically rich geography, marked by expansive forests, flowing rivers, and a scenic maritime frontier.

The northern regions are dominated by the Hoefen Forest, one of the kingdom’s most iconic natural landmarks. This immense woodland is home to a wide variety of native flora and fauna, and serves both as a symbol of Verdania’s natural heritage and a practical source of renewable resources, including timber, herbs, and game. The forest plays a vital role in environmental conservation efforts and is deeply embedded in national folklore, often associated with the kingdom's spiritual connection to the land.

In the southwestern reaches, the Wester Forest offers a contrasting yet equally important sylvan landscape. Though smaller than Hoefen, Wester is known for its dense canopy, tranquil clearings, and the ancient oaks that have stood for centuries. Both forests together reflect Verdania’s enduring policy of harmonizing industrial development with environmental stewardship, an ideal that is deeply rooted in both policy and cultural identity.

Running through the southern part of the kingdom is the Manchel River, a vital natural feature that forms part of the border with Agaritia. The river is not only a geographic marker but also a lifeline for agriculture and local economies, feeding the fertile southern plains and enabling irrigation-based farming. It also holds symbolic importance as a historic boundary, often referenced in royal decrees and national poems.

Off the western coastline, the Chisshen Archipelago extends into the Northern Sea. This chain of small islands provides a natural maritime boundary, shielding the mainland from the harsher currents of the northern waters and offering strategic and ecological significance. The archipelago is sparsely inhabited but highly valued for its pristine nature, seabird populations, and rugged coastal scenery. It also adds a distinct maritime dimension to Verdania’s otherwise forested terrain, enhancing its geographical variety and coastal charm.

Altogether, Verdania’s geography is a reflection of its dual identity: a kingdom steeped in natural grandeur and environmental reverence, yet firmly modern in its utilization of resources and land. From the deep woods of Hoefen to the island chains of Chisshen, every feature of the Verdanian landscape plays a role in shaping the nation's character, economy, and enduring connection to nature.

Politics

Government

Verdania is governed as a unitary constitutional monarchy, combining the enduring traditions of royal authority with the modern framework of a parliamentary democracy. The structure of the Verdanian government is defined by the Constitution, a foundational legal document that delineates the powers of the monarchy, the responsibilities of elected officials, and the rights of citizens. The system is designed to preserve national unity, continuity of governance, and a clear separation of powers, while upholding the cultural and symbolic significance of the Crown.

At the apex of the state stands the King, the reigning monarch and head of state. The King’s role is primarily ceremonial and symbolic, embodying the sovereignty, history, and unity of the Verdanian nation. Although the monarch formally appoints the Prime Minister, opens parliamentary sessions, and ratifies laws, these acts are performed in accordance with constitutional procedure and are subject to the decisions made by the elected government. The monarchy enjoys broad respect among the population and continues to play a central role in national ceremonies, diplomatic functions, and charitable patronage, representing the moral and cultural continuity of the kingdom.

Executive authority is vested in the Prime Minister, who serves as head of government and exercises effective political power. The Prime Minister is appointed by the King from among the members of Parliament, typically the leader of the majority party or coalition. The Prime Minister leads the Council of Ministers, composed of cabinet members responsible for various portfolios such as defense, foreign affairs, health, and infrastructure. Together, they oversee the day-to-day administration of the country, implement legislation, and guide national policy. The executive branch is accountable to Parliament and must retain its confidence to remain in power.

The legislative authority resides in the Parliament of Verdania, a unicameral body responsible for drafting and enacting laws, approving the national budget, and supervising the government. Parliament is composed of elected representatives who serve fixed terms and are chosen through universal suffrage and proportional representation, ensuring fair and broad political participation. The Speaker of Parliament presides over debates, manages legislative procedures, and ensures that parliamentary rules are respected.

Verdania’s judiciary is fully independent and operates under the protection of the Constitution. The Supreme Court, led by Chief Justice, is the highest judicial authority in the kingdom. It has the power to interpret constitutional law, review legislation, and arbitrate disputes between state institutions. The judiciary guarantees the protection of civil liberties, enforces legal accountability, and ensures that all governmental action complies with the law. Judges are appointed based on merit and judicial experience and are insulated from political influence to preserve impartiality.

Political parties

Verdania is regarded as a stable and well-functioning parliamentary democracy, operating under a constitutional monarchy that guarantees pluralism, civil rights, and political continuity. The kingdom’s political system fosters a mature and cooperative party environment, with power regularly alternating through peaceful elections and coalition-building, rather than confrontation or instability. The presence of an impartial monarchy provides symbolic unity above politics, allowing political discourse to remain focused on governance and policy rather than identity or factionalism. The country has a multi-party system, but most political activity revolves around three major parties that reflect the moderate and pragmatic political culture of Verdania:

  • The Conservative Party – A center-right party that upholds traditional values, monarchy, family, and national sovereignty. It favors fiscal conservatism, moderate economic liberalization, and a cautious approach to foreign policy. The RCP maintains close ties with rural communities, religious institutions, and veteran groups. While deeply respectful of the Crown, it supports limited constitutional reforms and promotes civic responsibility and order.
  • The Verdanian Social Democratic Union – A center-left party emphasizing social equity, public welfare, education, and workers’ rights. The VSDU supports progressive taxation, public healthcare, and environmental policy, while remaining respectful of Verdania’s cultural and religious traditions. It has strong support in urban centers, among educators, health workers, and younger voters. The party is also a major advocate for gender equality, digital modernization, and civil liberties.
  • The People’s Alliance – A centrist, liberal party that promotes pragmatic governance, market reform, and institutional transparency. The CPA often acts as a kingmaker in coalition governments and is known for its technocratic outlook and commitment to efficient administration, small business support, and innovation-led growth. It appeals to professionals, entrepreneurs, and the educated middle class, and supports a moderate pro-Edrobean outlook within the Edrobean Community.

Smaller parties, such as the Verdanian Green Federation, the Christian Civic League, and a few regional independents, also participate in elections and contribute to parliamentary diversity, but they rarely gain enough seats to form governments on their own. However, their voices are respected in debates, and parliamentary procedure ensures full inclusion of minority opinions.

All political parties in Verdania are subject to strict transparency laws, including public financing rules, campaign spending caps, and mandatory disclosures. The Electoral Commission, an independent body, oversees all elections and ensures that they are free, fair, and transparent, with high levels of voter trust and participation. Verdania’s political culture is marked by civility, consensus-building, and a rejection of extremism. Coalition governments are common, and even in opposition, parties cooperate on matters of national importance. Political debate is robust but respectful, and media freedom is constitutionally guaranteed, with public broadcasters offering equal airtime to all major political forces.

Administrative subdivisions

Verdania is administratively organized into five counties, including a distinct Capital County, forming the primary subnational framework for governance and public administration within the unitary structure of the state. These counties function as regional administrative zones designed to implement national policies, manage public services, and coordinate local affairs, but they do not possess legislative autonomy or independent political power. All authority ultimately flows from the central government, ensuring national cohesion and uniform governance standards throughout the kingdom.

Each county is administered by a County Commissioner, appointed by the national Ministry of the Interior. The commissioner oversees state functions at the regional level, such as education oversight, health coordination, infrastructure maintenance, emergency preparedness, and regional economic initiatives. Commissioners work in tandem with County Administrative Councils, composed of locally elected officials who advise on local priorities and represent community interests. These councils do not pass legislation but play an important consultative role, ensuring that the county's governance is responsive to its residents.

The Capital County of Uzdonstad functions as both a county and the national capital. It is home to the royal palace, the Parliament, the Supreme Court, and all major national ministries. Due to its political and symbolic importance, the capital enjoys a slightly elevated administrative status, with expanded funding and greater coordination between municipal and national authorities. The Mayor of Uzdonstad, elected by the city’s residents, works alongside the Capital Commissioner to manage both local services and national infrastructure concentrated in the capital region.

The other four counties—each with distinct geographic, economic, and cultural characteristics—are designed to balance population distribution, improve access to public services, and ensure consistent implementation of national policies. Counties often coordinate regional transport, environmental preservation programs, and emergency response systems across their municipalities. Within each county, municipalities serve as the lowest tier of governance, providing direct services to citizens such as waste management, primary education, urban planning, and community welfare. Municipal governments are elected and operate with limited budgetary discretion under the framework provided by county and national law.

This multi-tiered structure—central government → counties → municipalities—ensures administrative efficiency while preserving the unitary integrity of the Verdanian state. It allows the government to remain responsive and accessible at the local level, without fragmenting authority or enabling regionalist politics. Ultimately, Verdania’s administrative division reflects its broader model of orderly, centralized, and citizen-oriented governance, where regional identity coexists with strong national unity.

Foreign relations

Verdania’s foreign policy is grounded in a steadfast commitment to multilateralism, diplomacy, and principled sovereignty, reflecting its status as a constitutional monarchy with a stable democratic system. The kingdom supports a rules-based international order, actively participates in global institutions, and promotes peaceful cooperation, regional integration, and strategic neutrality in conflicts beyond its direct interests. As a founding member of the United Nations, Verdania is also a committed participant in the Global Reserve Fund, World Trade Network, Global Health Organization, and the International Energy Agency, where it is known for its measured voice and commitment to consensus-building.

Regionally, Verdania is an active member of the Edrobean Community and plays a constructive role in shaping continental policy, particularly on economic harmonization, environmental standards, and transport cooperation. It supports greater Edrobean integration, especially in areas of cross-border trade, digital connectivity, and civil infrastructure, while preserving its own national identity and traditional institutions.

One of Verdania’s most enduring bilateral successes is its close relationship with Jarnovia, with whom it shares both a land border and strong economic ties. The two nations have signed a series of economic cooperation treaties, trade facilitation pacts, and infrastructure agreements, including joint railway projects and energy corridor development. This relationship has fostered mutual prosperity and regional economic stability, with frequent cultural and academic exchanges reinforcing people-to-people ties.

In recent years, Verdania has also pursued strategic engagement with Valmirica, resulting in deepening economic ties and formalized military cooperation. The partnership with Valmirica, while carefully balanced to preserve Verdania’s sovereignty, includes joint defense planning, shared intelligence protocols, and cross-training exercises, aimed at enhancing regional security and deterring external threats. This alliance, though focused on defensive posture, marks a significant evolution in Verdania’s traditionally restrained defense diplomacy.

On the economic front, Verdania maintains strong commercial ties with Destenia and Comussania, its two most important trading partners. Destenia serves as a major supplier of industrial goods, advanced technology, and refined materials, while Comussania is a key market for Verdanian manufactured exports and agri-food products. These economic relationships are facilitated through structured bilateral agreements and Edrobean Community mechanisms, ensuring open market access and long-term stability.

Despite its alliances and economic interdependence, Verdania continues to emphasize its diplomatic independence and balanced international posture. It maintains embassies in all major capitals, abstains from interventionist policies, and advocates for peaceful resolution of disputes, climate diplomacy, and fair global governance. Its foreign service is known for discretion, professionalism, and a deep understanding of geopolitical nuance.

Military

Verdania maintains a small but professional and highly disciplined military, officially known as the Royal Armed Forces of Verdania. Structured around a doctrine of national defense, territorial integrity, and regional deterrence, the military is firmly under civilian control and operates within the boundaries of a constitutional monarchy that emphasizes peace, order, and multilateral cooperation. The Royal Armed Forces are not designed for power projection or offensive warfare, but rather for defensive readiness, alliance commitments, and domestic crisis response. The military is composed of three main branches:

  • The Royal Army serves as the principal ground force, responsible for defending national borders, supporting civil authorities in emergencies, and contributing to allied defensive coordination. The army is equipped with modern light and medium armored units, mobile artillery, and reconnaissance systems adapted for Verdania’s forested and coastal terrain. It operates on a volunteer basis, with a professional officer corps trained in strategy, logistics.
  • The Royal Air Force ensures the protection of national airspace and supports the army with airlift, surveillance, and tactical air defense capabilities. The fleet consists primarily of multi-role aircraft, transport planes, and rotary-wing assets, used for border patrol, humanitarian relief, and coordination with regional allies. Though limited in size, the Air Force benefits from shared training programs and intelligence exchanges, especially with Comussania, under a formal military cooperation agreement.
  • The Royal Navy, while administratively part of the defense structure, operates in close collaboration with the Ministry of Interior. It is tasked with patrolling Verdania’s western maritime border along the Northern Sea, ensuring maritime law enforcement, environmental monitoring, and emergency response in the Chisshen Archipelago. The Coast Guard also conducts anti-smuggling operations and search-and-rescue missions in coordination with Edrobean regional agencies.

Command of the military ultimately resides with the King, who serves as Commander-in-Chief, though all operational and strategic decisions are made by the Ministry of Defense, under the supervision of the Prime Minister and the parliamentary Defense Committee. Military leadership is apolitical, and all ranks are bound by strict codes of loyalty to the Constitution, rather than to any political office or party. Verdania does not maintain conscription; the armed forces are entirely voluntary and merit-based, with emphasis on training, ethics, and civic duty. The defense budget is moderate but consistent, reflecting the kingdom’s focus on sustainability, modernization, and readiness, rather than size or expansion.

Through its defense partnership with Comussania, Verdania participates in joint exercises, cybersecurity coordination, and regional contingency planning. However, the kingdom remains outside of any formal military alliance, preserving its sovereign decision-making and diplomatic neutrality. It also contributes small detachments to United Nations peacekeeping missions, particularly in medical support, logistics, and disaster response. In times of national emergency, the Royal Armed Forces can be deployed domestically to assist in natural disasters, public infrastructure protection, and national security crises, always under parliamentary authorization and judicial oversight.

Law enforcement

Law enforcement in Verdania is managed through a centralized, professional, and constitutionally regulated system, designed to ensure public safety, legal integrity, and the protection of civil liberties under the authority of the Ministry of Justice. Rooted in the kingdom’s long-standing legal tradition and reinforced by modern standards of democratic governance, Verdanian law enforcement prioritizes transparency, accountability, and community trust.

The core of the country’s law enforcement structure is the Verdanian National Police, a unified civilian force responsible for crime prevention, investigation, public order, and emergency response across all five counties of the kingdom. The police operate under a single command structure, with regional divisions managed by Provincial Commanders who report to the National Commissioner of Police, headquartered in Uzdonstad. The National Police is tasked with handling day-to-day enforcement duties, from traffic control and patrol to criminal investigations and anti-fraud operations. Specialized departments within the National Police include:

  • The Criminal Investigations Division, responsible for handling serious crimes, forensic analysis, and cross-county criminal coordination.
  • The Cybercrime and Financial Division, which combats digital threats, financial fraud, and economic offenses in cooperation with the Ministry of Finance.
  • The Public Order Division, which ensures peaceful management of demonstrations, political gatherings, and major public events, using non-lethal tools and de-escalation tactics as standard policy.
  • The Internal Affairs Division, which conducts oversight and internal investigations into police conduct, corruption, or abuse of power. It operates independently and reports to the Parliamentary Committee on Civil Rights and Justice.

Verdania also maintains a distinct Border and Customs Security Police, tasked with monitoring and protecting the kingdom’s land and maritime borders, particularly along the Chisshen Archipelago and the frontier with Comussania and Jarnovia. This force works in conjunction with the Coast Guard and Immigration Services to enforce entry laws, prevent smuggling, and manage lawful transit. All law enforcement personnel undergo extensive training in legal procedures, civil rights, community relations, and conflict resolution. Officers are required to complete continuing education throughout their careers and are subject to performance reviews, ethical audits, and public feedback mechanisms. The use of force is heavily regulated, with body camera requirements, strict reporting standards, and independent investigations in any case involving injury or death.

Municipalities maintain local police departments under the general supervision of the National Police, responsible for neighborhood policing, minor civil disputes, and municipal by-law enforcement. These local departments focus heavily on community engagement, working directly with residents to prevent crime, address concerns, and build public trust. Verdania’s legal system provides broad protections against unlawful detention, discrimination, and misuse of authority, and any citizen may file a complaint or request legal review of police conduct through the Office of the Citizens’ Order. Courts retain full authority to dismiss evidence or cases where police actions violate constitutional protections.

Economy

Verdania possesses a modern, diversified, and highly organized economy, ranking 13th in the world with a Gross Domestic Product of 440 billion DTR. Though relatively modest in population and size, the kingdom has established itself as an economic force through strategic trade relations, fiscal discipline, and technological modernization, all underpinned by the stability of its constitutional monarchy and democratic institutions. The Verdanian economy is defined by its commitment to balance—between market liberalism and social protections, between industrial development and environmental preservation, and between global integration and national resilience.

One of the pillars of Verdania’s economic strategy is its export-oriented industrial sector, which focuses on precision manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, electronics, and high-end mechanical engineering. Industrial hubs are concentrated around urban centers in the eastern and southern counties, where infrastructure has been tailored to support efficient production and transport logistics. The kingdom’s industries benefit from low corruption, a skilled workforce, and strong intellectual property protections, attracting both domestic innovation and international investment.

In the agricultural sector, Verdania emphasizes quality over quantity, specializing in organic produce, specialty grains, and dairy products, much of which is consumed domestically or exported under premium labels. While not agriculturally self-sufficient, the country enjoys strong food trade relations, especially with Destenia and Agaritia, from which it imports supplementary food items and raw materials. Agriculture is heavily regulated to protect environmental standards, animal welfare, and rural heritage.

Verdania’s energy sector is stable and sustainable. Although it lacks large fossil fuel reserves, the kingdom relies on clean energy imports from Comussania and Destenia, along with domestically produced hydropower and wind energy, to meet its national demand. The government invests significantly in green infrastructure and renewable energy innovation, supported by targeted subsidies and regional cooperation agreements. Verdania is also a member of the International Energy Agency, where it promotes balanced, science-based energy transitions.

The services sector plays a growing role in the national economy, particularly in banking, digital technology, education, tourism, and public administration. Verdania’s financial system is highly regulated and transparent, with a reputation for prudence, ethical lending practices, and fiscal conservatism. Uzdonstad, the capital, serves as a regional center for finance, legal services, and research institutions. Tourism, though modest, focuses on eco-tourism, cultural heritage, and religious pilgrimage, attracting visitors to the Hoefen Forest, historic monasteries, and the Chisshen coast.

Verdania trades extensively through the World Trade Network and is deeply integrated into the Edrobean Community’s internal market, exporting industrial goods, software solutions, and pharmaceuticals. It maintains strategic commercial partnerships with Destenia and Agaritia, its primary trade partners, while also enjoying robust economic treaties with Jarnovia and growing links with Comussania, including shared infrastructure projects and bilateral investments. Economically, Verdania is known for its strong regulatory institutions, low public debt, and stable currency—the Verdan Crown. The country consistently runs balanced budgets and invests surplus revenues in sovereign wealth reserves and long-term infrastructure. Employment is high, labor laws are protective, and the social safety net includes universal healthcare, public pensions, and generous family support schemes.

Demography

Verdania has a population of approximately 5,342,549 citizens, making it one of the smaller yet most socially cohesive nations in the Edrobean continent. The country’s demographic profile is characterized by stability, homogeneity, and high civic participation, reflecting a deeply embedded sense of national identity and a population shaped by shared language, faith, and historical continuity. Unlike many of its neighbors, Verdania has experienced minimal demographic disruption, thanks to its tight immigration controls, low emigration, and strong internal cohesion.

The median age in Verdania is 40 years, signaling a moderately aging society, though not yet facing the demographic pressures seen in some wealthier nations. Fertility rates hover around 1.8 children per woman, slightly below replacement level, but balanced by strong family support policies, generous maternity leave, and widespread access to childcare. The life expectancy is high, averaging 82 years, with women typically living longer than men by five to six years, a reflection of the kingdom’s excellent public healthcare system and low levels of social inequality.

Urbanization is modest by Edrobean standards, with approximately 68% of the population living in urban areas, mainly concentrated in the capital Uzdonstad and a few regional centers. Rural communities remain well-populated and are strongly supported through infrastructure investment, cultural preservation, and economic incentives that keep the countryside vibrant and productive. There is no significant urban-rural divide in political participation or access to public services.

Verdania maintains a rigid immigration framework, allowing only highly skilled individuals to obtain work permits, and offering citizenship only under rare, exceptional circumstances. As a result, natural population growth remains the sole engine of demographic change. The country has virtually no refugee intake, and emigration is minimal, with most Verdanian citizens preferring to remain within the kingdom due to the high quality of life, safety, and strong social fabric.

Education is compulsory and state-funded, with full literacy and high academic standards. The country invests heavily in vocational training, civic education, and language mastery, ensuring that every citizen is not only employable but also actively integrated into the national culture. The workforce is well-balanced across sectors, with increasing participation by women and older citizens, supported by flexible retirement schemes and lifelong learning programs.

Language

The official and only language of Verdania is Verdan, a highly standardized and deeply embedded tongue that serves as both the linguistic foundation and cultural soul of the kingdom. Used exclusively in government, education, media, judiciary, and public life, Verdan is not merely a tool of communication but a core symbol of national identity, continuity, and unity. Its widespread and uniform use across all regions of the country contributes significantly to Verdania’s reputation as the most culturally cohesive nation in Edrobe.

The language policy of Verdania is strictly monolingual. There are no recognized minority languages, and the use of foreign languages in official settings is not permitted. This policy reflects the state’s desire to preserve linguistic unity and ensure that all citizens participate in a shared civic culture. In public life—whether in courts, healthcare, education, or political institutions—only Verdan is used, reinforcing a clear and consistent national identity.

The Royal Language Academy plays a central role in regulating the evolution of Verdan. It oversees the creation of neologisms, adapts the language to modern contexts—especially in science and technology—and ensures that all official terminology remains culturally consistent and legally precise. It also sponsors literature, translation efforts, and language education abroad, reinforcing the prestige of Verdan as a language of governance, tradition, and refined expression.

Religion

Religion in Verdania is profoundly rooted in the Catholic tradition, with 95% of the population identifying as Christians, the overwhelming majority of whom adhere to the Catholic Church. The Church has played a foundational role in the spiritual, cultural, and moral development of the kingdom, and it continues to serve as a central institution in public life, though within the framework of a constitutional monarchy that guarantees religious freedom and separates church from state.

The Verdanian Catholic Church operates as a national branch of the global Catholic communion, loyal to the Vatican yet distinct in its close ties to the monarchy, history, and identity of the nation. The Church maintains a visible presence across the entire country, with cathedrals, parish churches, monasteries, and religious schools in every county. Major religious holidays such as Christmas, Easter, and the Feast of the Kingdom’s Patron Saints are national holidays, accompanied by official ceremonies, royal attendance, and widespread public participation.

The monarchy and the Church share a symbolic relationship, anchored in the national motto *“God shall defend us”*. Though the King holds no formal ecclesiastical authority, the Crown is regarded as a protector of the faith, and royal coronations and public events are traditionally accompanied by religious blessings and rites. The Royal Chapel in Uzdonstad serves as the spiritual heart of the kingdom, hosting state liturgies and national days of prayer.

Education and social services are areas where the Catholic Church plays a significant role. Many of Verdania’s most respected private schools, hospitals, and charitable institutions are run by Catholic orders, operating under state regulations but maintaining a religious ethos. Religious education is offered in public schools as an optional, values-based program, overseen jointly by the Ministry of Education and the Church, with secular alternatives available for non-participating students.

While Verdania is officially a secular state, its laws and public culture are clearly informed by Catholic social teaching, especially in areas such as family policy, ethical governance, and charitable responsibility. Political leaders often engage in respectful dialogue with Church authorities, particularly on matters involving education, bioethics, and public morality. However, the Church does not interfere in legislation or electoral politics, in keeping with constitutional limitations and public expectations of institutional separation.

The remaining 5% of the population includes minor Christian denominations, secular individuals, and a small number of adherents to other faiths. These groups are free to worship, organize, and practice their beliefs without interference, though their visibility and influence in public life remain limited. Proselytism is legally permitted, but cultural norms strongly favor religious continuity and discretion. The Episcopal Conference of Verdania governs the Church’s national affairs, coordinating dioceses, publishing doctrinal guidance, and managing seminary education. It also serves as a moral voice in national debates, especially on matters of social justice, the dignity of life, and care for the poor, aligning with the broader teachings of the global Catholic Church.

Ethnicities

Verdania is widely considered the most ethnically homogeneous country in the world, with 99% of its population identifying as ethnic Verdanians. This extraordinary level of homogeneity is the result of centuries of cultural continuity, geographic stability, and a deeply rooted national identity centered around language, faith, monarchy, and shared civic traditions. Ethnic Verdanians share a common heritage, speak the same language—Verdan—and practice a largely unified set of customs and values that have been passed down across generations. This unity is not only cultural but is also reflected in the legal, educational, and administrative systems, all of which are designed around a single, cohesive national identity.

The remaining 1% of the population consists primarily of foreign-born residents, most of whom are temporary or long-term professionals, entrepreneurs, and diplomatic personnel who have come to Verdania for business, specialized work, or intergovernmental collaboration. These individuals originate from across the Edrobean continent, including neighboring countries such as Destenia, Jarnovia, and Comussania. While welcomed for their economic contribution and expertise, this minority is not concentrated in any particular region and tends to reside in urban centers, particularly Uzdonstad, where international institutions and economic opportunities are most concentrated.

Verdania does not promote multiculturalism as a state policy. Instead, it practices a form of civic integration, in which all residents—regardless of origin—are expected to respect and adapt to Verdanian norms, language, and public conduct. While the 1% minority population faces no legal discrimination and enjoys full civil protections, there is little institutional accommodation for ethnic or cultural diversity, reflecting a national consensus in favor of cohesion, order, and cultural preservation.

Immigration policies are highly selective and tightly controlled, with stringent requirements for residency, naturalization, and family reunification. Permanent citizenship is rarely granted, and most foreign nationals reside in Verdania under special work or diplomatic visas, subject to regular review. The country’s approach to immigration is shaped by its desire to protect its national character, maintain social harmony, and ensure that any demographic changes occur gradually and with clear national benefit.

In summary, Verdania’s ethnic composition is nearly uniform by design and by history. The overwhelming majority of the population shares the same ancestry, language, and civic outlook, making Verdania a remarkably cohesive and culturally unified society. The small foreign minority is respected for its contributions but remains carefully integrated into a national model built on unity, tradition, and continuity.
