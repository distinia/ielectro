The Destenian Armed Forces are the military forces of Destenia, one of the most powerful militaries in the world. The Armed Forces are composed of these main branches: the Army, Navy, and Air Force. They operate under the civilian leadership of the Ministry of Defence and the strategic command of the High Command, led by the President as Supreme Commander, Minister of Defence, and Chief of High Command.

All major platforms are domestically designed and assembled; select subsystems, raw materials, and niche technologies are sourced from trusted foreign partners. The country’s military-industrial complex, supports continuous innovation in areas such as cyber warfare, missile technology, space defence, and autonomous systems.

The Army, the largest branch, handles land operations and territorial defence. The Navy secures maritime borders and operates a modern fleet, including submarines and an elite Marine Corps. The Air Force maintains air superiority through advanced fighters, bombers, and surveillance aircraft. Destenia is a recognized nuclear power maintaining a no-first-use posture and a credible second-strike deterrent.

The military is entirely voluntary and it promotes diversity and inclusion, with equal roles for women, ethnic minorities, religious groups, and individuals with disabilities. Political neutrality is enforced within the ranks, and all personnel pledge loyalty to the Constitution rather than any political entity. Training and education are managed by military schools and accademies, ensuring a highly professional soldiers. The military is organized into military commands, allowing rapid coordinated mobilization and territorial defence.

Organization

High Command

The High Command is the supreme military authority of the Destenian Armed Forces, responsible for the strategic coordination, joint command, and operational integration of all service branches. Functioning as the central pillar of Destenia’s defence architecture, the High Command ensures unity of doctrine, efficiency in execution, and rapid response to any form of threat—domestic or foreign. It operates under the joint leadership of the Supreme Commander, the Minister of Defence, and the Chief of the High Command, forming the highest decision-making body in the nation’s military hierarchy.

Oversight of the armed forces is constitutionally mandated, by the Supreme Commander, who is the President of Destenia, holds ultimate authority over all military strategy, symbolizing civilian control over the armed institutions of the state. The Minister of Defence serves as the principal executive for defence administration, bridging the gap between the High Command and the government. The Chief of the High Command, usually the highest-ranking military officer in Destenia, directs all operational forces, implements strategic directives, and supervises inter-branch coordination. The High Command also functions as the hub for international defence cooperation and joint military diplomacy, overseeing partnerships, training exchanges, and strategic coordination with allied nations. It maintains continuous communication with the National Security Council to ensure policy alignment in both peacetime and crisis.

Ministry of Defence

The Ministry of Defence stands as the central authority of national defence within the Destenia, responsible for the administration, coordination, and execution of all military policies and operations. It functions as the critical bridge between the civilian government and the Armed Forces, ensuring that the military remains under firm constitutional control while achieving maximum operational efficiency.

The Ministry directs the development of defence strategy, procurement systems, logistics, research and innovation, and personnel management, ensuring that every component of Destenia’s defence apparatus operates as a unified, disciplined, and strategically aligned system.

At its core, the Ministry is structured around a network of specialized offices, departments, and bureaus, each responsible for a distinct domain of military governance. The Office of the Minister of Defence provides overall leadership and policy direction, coordinating all branches of the Armed Forces. Supporting it, the Office of Strategic Operations oversees long-term planning, operational readiness, and wartime contingencies, ensuring preparedness across all scenarios. The Office of Military Procurement regulates defence contracts, acquisitions, and supply chains, safeguarding self-sufficiency and sustainability in armaments and logistics. Meanwhile, the Office of Veterans Affairs is entrusted with the welfare, healthcare, and reintegration of retired personnel, preserving the state’s commitment to those who have served.

The Ministry’s departments form the operational backbone of Destenia’s military structure. The Departments of the Army, Navy, and Air Force each oversee their respective branches under civilian supervision, ensuring both effectiveness and accountability. The Department of Joint Logistics coordinates inter-branch supply systems, transportation networks, and maintenance infrastructure, enabling seamless cooperation across all services. In parallel, the Department of Cyber Defence protects national digital infrastructure and leads offensive and defensive cyber operations, reflecting the evolving nature of modern warfare.

Complementing these structures, the Ministry’s bureaus guarantee standardization, discipline, and continuous professional development throughout the Armed Forces. The Bureau of Training and Doctrine defines military education and operational principles; the Bureau of Armaments and Standards ensures technological consistency and quality control; and the Bureau of Military Justice upholds discipline, legality, and ethical conduct within the ranks.

At the apex of military operations, the High Command, operating within the Ministry, serves as the supreme operational authority, directing combined-force strategy and ensuring unity of command across all branches. Through its centralized and integrated structure, the Ministry of Defence ensures the seamless alignment of national policy, industrial capability, and military power. It stands as the guardian of Destenia’s security architecture, balancing technological innovation, strategic foresight, and disciplined command, in order to preserve national sovereignty and sustain Destenia’s global military preeminence.

Academies

The Military Academies of Destenia represent the foundation of Destenia’s professional armed forces, serving as the principal institutions for training, education, and ideological formation of future military leaders. Designed to uphold the values of discipline, loyalty, and strategic excellence, these academies ensure that every officer—from cadet to general—embodies the Destenian doctrine of national service and constitutional fidelity. The academies operate under the supervision of the Ministry of Defence, maintaining standardized curricula across all branches of the Armed Forces. Their mission extends beyond tactical instruction—they cultivate officers who are not only skilled commanders, but also intellectual defenders of Destenia’s sovereignty and moral order.

Each branch of the Armed Forces maintains its own premier academy and military schools, reflecting its distinct operational culture and technical specialization. Admission into the academies is highly selective, requiring exceptional academic achievement, physical fitness, psychological stability, and moral integrity. Candidates undergo a rigorous entrance process, including national examinations, leadership evaluations, and personal interviews conducted by senior officers. Once admitted, cadets experience an intensive and comprehensive program combining academic education, military discipline, and ethical instruction. They study a wide range of subjects—strategic theory, history, science, politics, and technology—ensuring they emerge as both tacticians and critical thinkers. Training emphasizes physical endurance, combat readiness, and command capability, instilling the confidence and adaptability required for service in Destenia’s modern, multi-domain military environment.

Graduates of the academies are commissioned as officers in their respective branches and are expected to uphold the highest standards of professionalism and service. Many continue their education at advanced military institutions, preparing for leadership roles within the High Command or elite operational units. The academies also maintain active partnerships with civilian universities, scientific institutions, and defence industries, fostering innovation and research collaboration. Through their strict selection standards, elite training regimens, and integration with national defence strategy, the military academies ensure a continuous supply of capable, loyal, and intellectually rigorous officers. They stand not merely as schools of war, but as guardians of Destenia’s martial tradition, preserving the discipline, intelligence, and unity that define the Destenian Armed Forces.

Bases

The military bases of Destenia form the backbone of Destenia’s defence infrastructure, serving as the nerve centers of readiness, logistics, and command for all branches of the Destenian Armed Forces. Distributed strategically across Destenia’s territory and overseas holdings, these bases ensure that Destenia can mobilize rapidly, project power globally, and maintain constant operational preparedness. Each installation is designed to fulfill a specific purpose—whether as a combat hub, training ground, logistical hub, or research complex—but all operate under the unified doctrine of efficiency, self-sufficiency, and strategic integration. The military base network is organized into several functional tags:

  • Strategic Command Bases - These installations host the headquarters of major operational commands, including the High Command, the Ministry of Defence, and regional Army, Navy, and Air commands. Equipped with secure communication systems, underground command centers, and hardened data bunkers, they enable uninterrupted coordination during peacetime and war.
  • Army Bases - Spread across every military district, these bases house combat divisions, armored brigades, and support regiments. Facilities include live-fire ranges, urban warfare simulators, vehicle maintenance depots, and military academies for officer and NCO training.
  • Naval Bases - The Navy operates from a network of coastal fortresses, drydock facilities, and naval air stations located along the Grandic Sea and the Northern Sea.
  • Air Bases - The Air Force maintains a network of airbases across Destenia, each positioned for regional coverage and quick interception capability. These bases include underground hangars, radar command towers, and automated drone control hubs, ensuring both offensive projection and defensive airspace monitoring. In peacetime, they also host search and rescue and humanitarian missions.
  • Research and Intelligence Bases - These are the technological heart of the Armed Forces. It conducts advanced research in AI warfare, stealth systems, space propulsion, and energy weapons. Many are classified installations located in remote regions, heavily guarded and accessible only to authorized personnel. Their innovations sustain Destenia’s military-industrial superiority and ensure that every generation of weaponry remains domestically produced and globally competitive.
  • Foreign and Joint Bases - Though Destenia does not maintain permanent foreign garrisons, it operates rotational deployments with partners within the Edrobean Community and the United Nations. These facilities host joint exercises, intelligence cooperation, and peacekeeping operations, reflecting Destenia’s commitment to collective security and strategic diplomacy.

Joint commands

The Joint Commands of the Destenian Armed Forces represent the unified operational structure that integrates all service branches—Army, Navy, and Air Force—under a single strategic framework. These commands ensure full-spectrum coordination across land, sea, air, space, cyber, and nuclear domains, enabling the Destenian Republic to maintain rapid response capability, strategic balance, and technological supremacy.

Functioning directly under the High Command, the Joint Commands are responsible for regional defence, inter-branch cooperation, intelligence fusion, and special operations. Each Command operates autonomously in its assigned sphere but remains fully synchronized through the Joint Operations Directorate, ensuring that the Destenian Armed Forces act as one cohesive system in peace and war alike.

Main Regional and Functional Commands

  • Northern Theater Command – Headquartered in Fort Valostia, this command oversees the defence of Destenia’s northern frontiers. It is responsible for cold-weather operations, border security, and the coordination of rapid response units in the highlands and northern plains. The Northern Theater Command also manages the region’s air defence grid and coastal radar installations.
  • Southern Theater Command – Based in Port Serana, it commands the southern military districts, maritime zones, and overseas operations. The Southern Theater Command supervises amphibious forces, marine detachments, and coastal defence regiments, coordinating joint Army-Navy operations along the Grandic Ocean.
  • Eastern Theater Command – Operating from Fort Litorato, this command secures the eastern approaches and industrial heartland of Destenia. It plays a vital role in homeland defence, logistics coordination, and air defence readiness, housing several armored and mechanized divisions ready for rapid deployment.
  • Western Theater Command – Centered at Fort Corviano, the Western Theater Command manages operations across the western territories and frontier borders. It specializes in mechanized warfare, counterinsurgency operations, and tactical air support coordination with the Air Force’s western airbases.

Strategic and Specialized Commands

  • Space Command – Responsible for Destenia’s orbital and extra-atmospheric defence, the Space Command directs military satellites, anti-satellite systems, and orbital reconnaissance platforms. It maintains real-time surveillance, communication, and missile-tracking capabilities through the Orbital Defence Network, ensuring Destenia’s dominance in the space domain.
  • Logistical Command – The Logistical Command coordinates the nationwide military supply network, including transport, maintenance, and energy distribution. It operates under the Ministry of Defence but directly supports all field operations, guaranteeing uninterrupted supply lines, ammunition reserves, and strategic fuel management across all theaters.
  • Army Intelligence – The intelligence branch of the Destenian Army, focusing on tactical reconnaissance, battlefield surveillance, and counter-espionage within ground operations. It cooperates closely with the Ministry of National Security and the Joint Intelligence Committee for threat analysis and army security.
  • Air Force Intelligence – The aerial intelligence arm, specializing in aerial reconnaissance, satellite imaging, and electronic warfare interception. It provides targeting data, radar surveillance, and airspace threat assessments, supporting both conventional and strategic operations.
  • Navy Intelligence – The naval intelligence division is responsible for maritime surveillance, naval counterintelligence, and the monitoring of global sea lanes. It manages undersea sensor networks and reconnaissance drones to ensure maritime situational awareness and operational secrecy.
  • Special Forces – The Special Forces unites all elite units across the branches, including Army Rangers, Marine Raiders, Airborne Commandos, and Counterterrorism Teams. Tasked with direct action, covert operations, hostage rescue, and high-risk reconnaissance, this command represents Destenia’s most flexible and lethal rapid-response force.

Reserve and Strategic Commands

  • Army Reserve – Manages the training, mobilization, and integration of reserve soldiers. It ensures that reserve divisions remain combat-ready, equipped, and synchronized with active-duty formations for rapid national mobilization.
  • Air Force Reserve – Oversees reserve airmen, pilots, and maintenance crews. It maintains standby squadrons, training programs, and aircraft readiness to reinforce active commands during extended operations or emergencies.
  • Navy Reserve – Composed of reservists, this command ensures the naval reserve fleet and auxiliary ships are operationally maintained. It also trains merchant mariners and support personnel for naval logistics and coastal defence duties.
  • Strategic Missile Command – The Strategic Missile Command is tasked with managing Destenia’s arsenal of intercontinental and intermediate-range ballistic missiles. It ensures the readiness, maintenance, and command integrity of the nation’s missile silos, mobile launchers, and underground deterrence complexes.
  • Strategic Nuclear Command – The apex of Destenia’s defence hierarchy, the Strategic Nuclear Command (SNC) maintains direct control over Destenia’s nuclear triad—land-based ICBMs, submarine-launched ballistic missiles (SLBMs), and strategic bombers. Operating under strict civilian oversight, it guarantees the credibility of Destenia’s no-first-use doctrine and the absolute security of its second-strike capability.

Army

The Army is the largest and most strategically vital branch of the Destenian Armed Forces, entrusted with the execution of land-based military operations and the defence of territorial sovereignty. As the backbone of Destenia’s military power, it is tasked with securing national borders, conducting counterinsurgency missions, and participating in peacekeeping deployments when mandated by state policy. With an arsenal that includes next-generation battle tanks, precision artillery systems, armored personnel carriers, and cutting-edge infantry weaponry, the Army is structured to perform across diverse terrains and in multi-domain operational environments.

Organizationally, the Army is composed of multiple divisions, including mechanized infantry, armored corps, airborne units, and special operations brigades. These units are trained for high mobility and rapid deployment, enabling Destenia to respond decisively to threats both within and beyond its borders. The branch also incorporates cyber and electronic warfare units, granting it the ability to counter hybrid and non-traditional threats, ensuring superiority in conventional and asymmetric warfare.

Civilian oversight of the Army is exercised through the Head of the Department of the Army, a senior official within the Ministry of Defence. This department oversees strategic planning, force development, training systems, and logistical support, working in tandem with the Chief of the Army, who commands all land forces and is responsible for operational effectiveness, battlefield doctrine, and mission readiness. The department also supervises recruitment, military education, and modernization initiatives, aiming to keep the Army technologically and structurally superior.

Beyond combat readiness, the Army contributes significantly to national resilience, frequently participating in disaster relief, emergency management, and infrastructure recovery operations during peacetime. It also plays a key role in international cooperation, engaging in joint training exercises and coalition readiness programs with allied states to ensure interoperability and strategic alignment.

Navy

The Navy is one of the most strategically vital branches of the Destenian Armed Forces, responsible for the protection of maritime sovereignty, the defence of national sea lanes, and the projection of naval power beyond Destenia’s territorial waters. As Destenia’s shield and spear at sea, the Navy ensures continuous control over trade routes, strategic chokepoints, and coastal defences. With a fleet that combines technological precision, logistical endurance, and offensive capability, the Navy maintains full-spectrum dominance in both blue-water and littoral operations, ensuring Destenia’s presence and readiness across the world’s oceans.

Organizationally, the Navy is structured into several operational fleets, including surface combatants, submarine forces, naval aviation, and the Destenian Marine Corps. The Surface Fleet is composed of nuclear-powered aircraft carriers, guided-missile destroyers, frigates, and amphibious assault ships, while the Submarine Command operates both ballistic missile submarines and attack submarines, forming a crucial component of Destenia’s strategic deterrence capability. The Marine Corps serves as the Navy’s expeditionary arm, trained for amphibious warfare, coastal defence, and rapid deployment missions. In time of war, the Destenian Coast Guard is placed under the Navy’s command, expanding its maritime surveillance, patrol, and logistics capacity to ensure total control over the nation’s territorial waters and shipping corridors.

Civilian oversight of the Navy is exercised through the Head of the Department of the Navy, a senior official within the Ministry of Defence. This department oversees naval strategy, fleet modernization, training programs, and shipbuilding policy, ensuring continuous technological superiority. The Chief of the Navy commands all maritime forces, directing operational readiness, doctrine development, and global deployments in coordination with the High Command. Together, these institutions ensure that every vessel, crew, and system operates under unified strategic command and in alignment with national defence priorities.

Beyond combat missions, the Navy plays a key role in humanitarian operations, disaster relief, and international maritime cooperation. It regularly participates in joint exercises with allied nations, enhancing interoperability, crisis response, and coalition readiness. The Navy also contributes to scientific research, environmental monitoring, and anti-piracy operations, reflecting Destenia’s commitment to both security and global stability.

Air Force

The Air Force is the aerial warfare branch of the Destenian Armed Forces, tasked with securing air superiority, conducting strategic bombing, and providing aerospace defence across all theaters of operation. As the vanguard of Destenia’s technological innovation, the Air Force embodies Destenia’s commitment to precision, deterrence, and rapid global reach. It ensures the protection of national airspace, supports ground and naval forces through coordinated strikes, and sustains continuous readiness to project power far beyond Destenia’s borders.

Organizationally, the Air Force is composed of tactical, strategic, and support commands, each responsible for distinct mission profiles. The Tactical Air Command oversees fighter and interceptor squadrons that maintain air dominance through fleets of fifth and sixth-generation multirole aircraft, stealth bombers, and unmanned aerial systems (UAS). The Strategic Air Command controls long-range bombers, aerial refueling units, and nuclear-capable delivery platforms, forming a critical component of Destenia’s deterrence triad. The Aerospace Defence Command monitors and defends national airspace through radar networks, surface-to-air missile systems, and orbital surveillance platforms. Specialized branches within the Air Force manage electronic warfare, cyber-defence integration, and space-based reconnaissance.

Civilian oversight of the Air Force is exercised through the Head of the Department of the Air Force, a senior official within the Ministry of Defence, responsible for strategic planning, research and development, and aviation infrastructure. The Chief of the Air Force commands all aerial and aerospace units, ensuring operational readiness, doctrinal advancement, and pilot training excellence. Together, these offices oversee aircraft production programs, resource allocation, and modernization projects in collaboration with Defence Aerospace and other domestic defence industries, maintaining strategic autonomy while sourcing limited specialized components from trusted partners.

The Air Force also fulfills vital roles in non-combat operations, including humanitarian airlifts, medical evacuations, search and rescue missions, and disaster response. It regularly conducts joint operations with the Army and Navy, promoting interoperability and multi-domain command integration. Airmen are trained at the Destenian Air Academy, which emphasizes aeronautical science, strategic leadership, and combat discipline. Through continuous modernization, global reach, and a culture of innovation, the Destenian Air Force stands as a pillar of national defence.

Personnel

Political restriction

The Armed Forces are constitutionally mandated to remain strictly independent from political influence, ensuring that their sole allegiance is to Destenia and its Constitution. Upon enlistment, every member of the military takes a formal oath of service, affirming their unwavering commitment to the legal order and the defence of national sovereignty. The oath reads:

"I pledge to the Destenian Republic, to defend its sovereignty, its Constitution, and its citizens from all enemies, foreign and domestic. I vow to obey the lawful orders of the Supreme Commander and of my appointed superiors. I shall serve with honor, discipline, and integrity"

Active-duty personnel may be members of political parties, as long as they remain politically neutral in uniform and in service. They are strictly prohibited from political activity while in active duty, including campaigning, endorsing candidates, attending rallies, or wearing party symbols. Military personnel must not hold any party position or engage in partisan actions while serving. This ensures the armed forces remain loyal to the Constitution, not to factions, and preserves their discipline, unity, and institutional integrity.

Despite these restrictions, military personnel retain core democratic rights. They are allowed to vote in elections and make private financial contributions to political campaigns, provided such actions are conducted discreetly and do not compromise the perception of military neutrality. However, any form of overt political expression, public or online, is considered a violation of military conduct regulations and may lead to disciplinary action, including suspension or dismissal from service.

These policies are designed to prevent the politicization of the armed forces, safeguard their professionalism, and uphold their exclusive mission: to serve Destenia, not any political faction. By remaining loyal to the Constitution alone, the Destenian Armed Forces preserve public trust, maintain institutional cohesion, and ensure that national defence is executed with discipline, unity, and constitutional fidelity.

Recruitment

Recruitment into the Armed Forces of Destenia is conducted on a strictly voluntary basis, reflecting Destenia’s commitment to maintaining a professional, elite, and highly disciplined military force. Conscription is not practiced, as Destenia relies on a selective and rigorous enlistment process to ensure that only individuals with the highest aptitude, character, and potential are admitted into service. This model supports Destenia’s doctrine of building a capable and loyal force aligned with its global military supremacy.

The Armed Forces recruit both citizens and non-citizens, offering foreign nationals a direct path to Destenian citizenship upon completing five years of honorable service. This policy has become a key element in expanding the military’s global talent pool, attracting skilled and motivated individuals from abroad. Foreign enlistees undergo the same training, screening, and evaluations as citizen recruits, ensuring a unified sense of duty and full integration into the military structure. Upon completing their service term, they are granted full citizenship, along with all legal rights and national benefits. Foreign nationals are excluded from nuclear forces, strategic missile commands, and top-tier intelligence postings.

The Ministry of Defence invests heavily in modern recruitment strategies, employing multimedia campaigns designed to glorify service and elevate the prestige of the military profession. Propaganda films, digital content, posters, and targeted social campaigns highlight the Armed Forces’ technological superiority, combat readiness, and global influence, presenting military service as a path to honor, adventure, economic security, and national pride. These campaigns are tailored to youth demographics, particularly those seeking purpose, challenge, and advancement in life.

Educational partnerships play a major role in cultivating the next generation of military leaders. Schools and universities collaborate with the Armed Forces to provide officer training programs, scholarships, and career mentorship for high-performing students. Promising candidates with exceptional academic or physical capabilities are fast-tracked into elite training academies, where they receive early exposure to military life. National recruitment events, simulation exercises, and military immersion programs are regularly held to engage and inspire potential enlistees.

The selection process is highly competitive, involving physical fitness trials, psychological assessments, aptitude tests, and extensive background checks. Recruits must demonstrate discipline, mental resilience, and analytical ability, ensuring that they can endure the demands of military life and adapt to high-pressure environments. Those who pass the selection phase are inducted into basic training, where they are taught combat tactics, military protocols, and branch-specific specializations. In addition to traditional recruitment channels, Destenia utilizes advanced AI algorithms to analyze online behavior, career interests, and personality profiles, identifying potential recruits with high compatibility for military roles. Social media platforms are leveraged to disseminate targeted recruitment content, allowing for personalized outreach across diverse demographic groups and geographic areas.

Ranks

The ranking structure of the Armed Forces embodies a strict system of hierarchy, discipline, and meritocracy, ensuring that promotion and authority are earned through performance, experience, and leadership capability. This structure is foundational to maintaining command efficiency, cohesion, and the professional identity of the military. All branches—the Army, Navy, and Air Force—adhere to a unified rank framework, with branch-specific variations that reflect specialized operational roles.

At the base are the enlisted personnel, who form the core of military operations. These individuals undergo intensive training in combat, logistics, engineering, communications, and support services. They execute tactical tasks, maintain equipment functionality, and serve as the operational foundation of the armed forces. Enlisted members have opportunities to ascend through the ranks via technical specialization, field excellence, and leadership initiatives.

Above them are the non-commissioned officers (NCOs), the backbone of leadership in daily operations. NCOs bridge the gap between enlisted personnel and commissioned officers, enforcing discipline, directing tactical operations, and mentoring junior troops. Their authority is earned through field credibility, and they are entrusted with maintaining morale, cohesion, and unit effectiveness in high-pressure environments. NCOs are deeply respected and considered institutional pillars of the armed forces.

Commissioned officers are charged with strategic planning, command authority, and policy implementation. Officers begin their careers as lieutenants after graduating from military academies or officer training programs. Through years of command experience, continuous education, and operational success, they rise to higher ranks such as majors, colonels, and eventually generals or admirals, depending on the branch. Senior officers are responsible for commanding brigades, fleets, or air wings, overseeing joint operations, and contributing to national security strategy.

At the summit of the structure lies the High Command, composed of the Supreme Commander (President of Destenia), the Minister of Defence, and the Chief of High Command. These leaders hold the ultimate responsibility for coordinating all branches, formulating grand strategy, and maintaining national readiness and deterrence.

The ranking system fosters a culture of excellence and upward mobility. Through structured career progression paths, leadership development, and advanced military education, service members are empowered to rise through the ranks and serve at increasing levels of responsibility. This structure ensures that the Armed Forces of Destenia remain a professional, loyal, and strategically capable institution, committed to defending the sovereignty and global standing of Destenia.

Weapons and equipment

Destenia’s military is built on a foundation of total self-sufficiency, with all weapons systems, vehicles, and defence platforms designed and manufactured primarily within national borders. This independence is a cornerstone of both Destenia’s sovereign defence doctrine and its economic strategy, enabling full control over military procurement, innovation, and deployment without reliance on foreign suppliers. The national defence industry is spearheaded by a combination of state-owned and private contractors, including Destenian Defence Industries, D-Munitions Ltd, and Defence Aerospace—companies that produce the full spectrum of armored vehicles, warships, fighter aircraft, missile systems, and infantry weapons used by the Armed Forces.

A key focus of modern defence policy is cyberwarfare, considered a strategic domain equal to land, sea, air, and space. The Department of Cyber Defence, in coordination with dedicated cyber commands, oversees national cyber operations, safeguarding military and civilian networks through AI-driven cybersecurity platforms, electronic warfare systems, and offensive cyber capabilities. These systems allow Destenia to preemptively neutralize digital threats, disrupt enemy infrastructure, and maintain information dominance in every theater of conflict.

As a recognized nuclear power maintaining a no-first-use posture and a credible second-strike deterrent, Destenia maintains a powerful nuclear deterrent. The national arsenal, developed in secure, classified facilities, includes intercontinental ballistic missiles, strategic cruise missiles, and hypersonic delivery systems. Nuclear plants in Litorato and Valostia provide the backbone of the nuclear program, supporting both energy needs and weapons refinement. The state adheres to a strict no-first-use doctrine, but guarantees unrivaled second-strike capabilities, making its deterrence posture credible, secure, and globally credible.

The doctrine of the Armed Forces emphasizes continuous modernization, with sustained investment in next-generation technologies, including stealth systems, robotic warfare, AI battlefield integration, autonomous vehicles, and directed-energy weapons. Every aspect of the military—from infantry squads to orbital and deep-space surveillance platforms—is subject to ongoing enhancement, ensuring that Destenia remains operationally superior in every dimension of modern warfare.

Budget

The Destenian Armed Forces are supported by a defence budget equivalent to 3.2% of the national GDP, amounting to 178.6B DTR, making it one of the largest military budgets in the world. This level of funding reflects Destenia’s unwavering commitment to military supremacy, national security, and global strategic influence. The budget is meticulously structured to provide a balanced and future-oriented investment across all pillars of defence—personnel, technology, readiness, and strategic capabilities.

A substantial portion of the budget is allocated to personnel welfare, ensuring the recruitment, retention, and professional development of a highly skilled and motivated force. Competitive salaries, housing allowances, healthcare coverage, and educational benefits are provided to all service members and extended to veterans, reinforcing military service as a prestigious and secure career path. The military also invests in training academies, advanced schools, and career development programs to cultivate leadership and adaptability at every level of the force.

A major focus of the budget is directed toward the modernization and procurement of advanced weapon systems, vehicles, and technological platforms. Continuous upgrades are made to aircraft fleets, naval warships, armored vehicles, and long-range missile systems to maintain operational dominance. The Destenian Nuclear Agency, which manages Destenia’s nuclear deterrent, receives significant funding to develop and sustain ICBMs, precision-strike systems, and second-strike infrastructure.

Research and development (R\&D) is a vital component of defence spending, ensuring Destenia remains technologically superior. Major investments are directed toward hypersonic weaponry, AI-enhanced battlefield systems, autonomous combat drones, and quantum computing for secure communications and data processing. Military-industrial partnerships ensure that innovation is anchored domestically, with state-funded laboratories working in tandem with private defence contractors to produce next-generation indigenous technologies.

Another critical area of expenditure is operational readiness and logistics. Funding supports a robust network of military bases, supply depots, and transport systems, ensuring that the Armed Forces maintain a high state of preparedness. Fuel reserves, ammunition stockpiles, strategic resource caches, and deployment infrastructure are all maintained at optimal levels. Emphasis is placed on mobility, rapid deployment, and strategic coordination, enabling Destenia to respond swiftly to any threat, whether domestic crisis or international escalation.

Although Destenia does not maintain foreign military bases, its defence budget ensures the existence of a fortified homeland security network. This includes coastal fortifications, missile defence systems, surveillance arrays, and integrated air defence platforms, all designed to protect national borders from air, sea, space, or cyber intrusion.

Foreign cooperation

Destenia’s foreign military cooperation is shaped by its doctrine of non-alignment, sovereign independence, and technological leadership, allowing Destenia to engage widely without entering formal alliance commitments or surrendering operational control. Within the Edrobean Community (EC), Destenia remains the principal architect of the region’s collective security framework, exercising decisive influence through the Edrobean Military Committee (EMC). The EMC serves as a council of Chiefs of Staff from all member states, convening for strategic dialogue, interoperability planning, and defence standardization, but deliberately refraining from commanding any integrated armed force. Destenia’s participation therefore centers on coordination, logistics, and technological unification—never on the surrender of national sovereignty.

Each year, the Community organizes a series of joint military exercises, hosted in rotation among member states. These exercises test interoperability, rapid deployment, cyber coordination, and counterterrorism readiness, allowing Destenia’s forces to train alongside other Edrobean militaries without forming a joint standing army. The Destenian Armed Forces often lead the planning and command simulation components, contributing both logistical expertise and advanced systems for command, control, and communications. Through these drills, Destenia ensures that Edrobe’s militaries can operate together in emergencies while preserving full national command independence.

Beyond the continent, the Destenian Navy operates with complete strategic autonomy, maintaining a global maritime presence to combat international trafficking, piracy, and illegal arms transport. Its patrol fleets maintain a persistent presence on major sea checkpoints, conducting continuous security operations independent of both the EC and the United Nations. These missions, grounded in international maritime law but directed exclusively by Destenia, symbolize Destenia’s role as a guarantor of global sea-lane security.

Although Destenia rejects participation in any United Nations peacekeeping operations, it remains actively engaged in bilateral military exercises and training exchanges with selected strategic partners, notably Fesia and Lamberia. These collaborations focus on naval coordination, anti-piracy tactics, and aerospace defence drills, strengthening mutual trust without creating binding defence commitments.

The cornerstone of Destenia’s foreign military cooperation lies in technology, intelligence, and defence industry partnerships. Through firms such as Destenian Defence Industries, D-Munitions Ltd, and Defence Aerospace, Destenia exports cutting-edge systems, shares intelligence data, and licenses advanced platforms to allied states. Collaborative projects in cybersecurity, missile defence, and space reconnaissance are carried out under strict oversight to preserve strategic parity and prevent dependency.

This approach—anchored in operational independence, selective interoperability, and controlled technological cooperation—has made Destenia the defence powerhouse of Edrobe and a global model of disciplined sovereignty. By integrating where beneficial, cooperating where strategic, and commanding where necessary, Destenia ensures that its military partnerships serve one purpose alone: the preservation of national autonomy and global stability through strength.
