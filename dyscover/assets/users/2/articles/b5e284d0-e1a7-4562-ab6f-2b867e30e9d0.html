Jarnovia, officially the Kingdom of Jarnovia, is a sovereign country located in northeastern Edrobe, bordered by Verdania to the southwest, Comussania to the southeast, Kratonia to the east, and the Northern Sea to the west and north. Covering an area of 125,473 km² and with a population of approximately 5 million, Jarnovia is a unitary constitutional monarchy. Its capital and largest city is Wennestadt.

The country is governed under a parliamentary system led by King Erik III, with executive authority vested in the Prime Minister, currently Lars Johansson, and legislative authority held by the Royal Assembly. The judicial branch is headed by the Supreme Court, presided over by Oskar Nilsson. The Speaker of the Assembly is Freja Svensson. Jarnovia's government combines traditional monarchical symbolism with modern democratic practices and is recognized globally as one of the most stable and transparent democracies.

Jarnovia's official language is Jarnovian, spoken natively by the vast majority of its ethnically Jarnovian population, which constitutes 85% of citizens. The remaining 15% includes Verdanians, Comussans, Kratonians, and others, most of whom are fully integrated into civic life. The dominant religion is Protestant Christianity, specifically the Jarnovian Reformed Church, followed by 93% of the population. Religious traditions play a prominent role in national identity and ceremonial life, especially in conjunction with the monarchy.

The kingdom has a highly developed economy, ranking 14th globally with a GDP of 430 billion DTR. Its economy is driven by services, high-tech manufacturing, renewable energy, and a strong social market system. Jarnovia maintains low unemployment, high education and healthcare standards, and is a leader in green innovation and digital governance. The Jarnovian Krone is the national currency.

Jarnovia is a member of numerous international and regional organizations, including the United Nations, Edrobean Community, Global Reserve Fund, International Tribunal for Justice, World Trade Network, Global Health Organization, and International Energy Agency. Its foreign policy is characterized by support for peace, democracy, and multilateral cooperation.

Etymology

The name Jarnovia derives from the ancient Jarnic root “Jarn”, meaning “iron”, symbolizing both the rugged terrain of the kingdom and the enduring strength of its people. The suffix “-ovia” is a traditional territorial marker in old Edrobean languages, signifying a land or domain. Together, "Jarnovia" can be interpreted as “Land of Iron”—a name historically associated with the country’s early blacksmithing culture, fortified hill towns, and its people's reputation for resilience, discipline, and loyalty.

According to Jarnovian legend, the term was first used by highland tribes to describe the mountainous heartland where early kings forged both weapons and kingdoms. The name gained official use during the unification period under the first royal dynasty and became a national identity marker following the coronation of King Erik I, who sought to emphasize unity, faith, and strength in the face of external pressures.

Geography

Jarnovia is located in the northeastern part of Edrobe, bordered by the Northern Sea to the west and north, giving it an extensive coastline and a long maritime tradition. To the southwest, it shares a border with Verdania, to the southeast with Comussania, and to the east with Kratonia, forming a strategic position between the Edrobean heartland and the northern maritime corridor.

Geographically, Jarnovia is defined by vast plains, rolling hills, and dense temperate forests, making it one of the greenest and most fertile countries in the region. The central lowlands, known as the Erlund Plain, serve as the agricultural and population heartland, supporting both major urban centers and traditional farming communities.

The country is interlaced with several rivers, the most prominent being the River Halden, which flows from the Vandral Highlands in the east and cuts across the capital, Wennestadt, before reaching the Bay of Aurin in the northwest. Other notable rivers include the Kellen, a tributary of the Halden in the southeast, and the Brisna, which forms part of the southern border with Verdania.

Jarnovia is home to several significant lakes, most notably Lake Nymar, located in the western forested region and known for its crystal waters and royal estates, and Lake Elsja, situated near the Comussanian border and used as a natural reservoir and strategic waterway.

Although primarily flat and forested, Jarnovia’s eastern frontier is marked by the Vandral Highlands, a modest mountain range rich in iron and limestone. The highest peak, Mount Varstein (2,114 m), is a symbolic site of national pride, frequently referenced in Jarnovian folklore and Christian iconography as a place of divine revelation and royal pilgrimage.

The country’s abundant forests—particularly the Velbra Woods in the north and the Korstwald Forest in the central basin—are protected as part of Jarnovia’s strong environmental policies and serve both ecological and cultural functions. Wildlife reserves, wooden churches, and ancient shrines are scattered throughout these areas, reinforcing the kingdom’s close connection to nature and tradition.

Politics

Government

Jarnovia is a unitary constitutional monarchy, combining the enduring traditions of royal sovereignty with the principles of modern parliamentary governance. The country’s political framework is defined by a monarchic institution at the core, supported by a democratic legislature, a representative executive, and an independent judiciary. The structure reflects a deep respect for heritage, religion, and the idea of the Crown as the symbol of national unity and divine continuity.

The King of Jarnovia, serves as the head of state and supreme commander of the armed forces. While the monarch’s role is largely ceremonial in daily governance, the King retains important constitutional powers such as appointing the Prime Minister, dissolving the legislature under certain conditions, ratifying laws, and serving as a guardian of the Constitution and the Church. The King is revered as a unifying figure who embodies the spiritual and historical identity of the Jarnovian people.

Executive authority is exercised by the Prime Minister, who acts as the head of government. The Prime Minister is appointed by the King, typically from the party or coalition holding a majority in the Royal Assembly, and is responsible for forming a Cabinet of Ministers. The Cabinet oversees domestic and foreign policy, public administration, and national defense, operating with the support of a professional civil service and a deeply rooted tradition of bureaucratic integrity. The Prime Minister is accountable to both the Assembly and the Crown.

Legislative power rests with the Royal Assembly, a unicameral body composed of elected representatives serving five-year terms. The Assembly is presided over by the Speaker of the House, who manages legislative sessions, mediates debates, and ensures procedural order. The Assembly debates and passes laws, approves the national budget, ratifies treaties, and can vote no confidence in the government. Despite the symbolic prominence of the monarchy, the Assembly plays a substantive role in shaping national policy through a parliamentary consensus model rooted in compromise and constitutional restraint.

The judicial branch is led by the Supreme Court, headed by the Supreme Justice. The Court is tasked with interpreting the Constitution, ensuring the legality of executive and legislative actions, and protecting individual rights under the law. Judicial independence is constitutionally guaranteed, and appointments to the bench are made through a consultative process involving both the Crown and the Assembly.

Political parties

Jarnovia is widely regarded as one of the strongest and most stable democracies in the world, characterized by transparent institutions, high civic participation, and a balanced multiparty system. The political landscape is centered around three main parties, which have alternated in government over the years and have at times formed coalition administrations to ensure stable governance and legislative cooperation.

  • Jarnovian People's Party – A center-right, liberal-conservative party, the JPP advocates for free-market principles, individual liberties, Christian democratic values, and moderate monarchism. It has traditionally been close to the Crown and the Church, emphasizing social cohesion, fiscal responsibility, and cultural preservation. The JPP is popular among rural voters, small business owners, and traditionalist segments of society.
  • Social Union of Jarnovia – A center-left social democratic party, the SUJ promotes progressive taxation, expanded welfare programs, environmental protection, and workers’ rights. It appeals primarily to urban voters, labor groups, and the academic community. While historically critical of royal privilege, the SUJ accepts the monarchy within the constitutional framework and focuses on building a more equitable and inclusive society.
  • Jarnovian Green Alliance – A centrist-environmentalist party, the JGA focuses on sustainable development, climate policy, digital innovation, and civil rights. Though smaller than the JPP and SUJ, it often plays a kingmaker role in forming governments, having entered coalition agreements with both major parties in the past. It enjoys strong support among youth, professionals, and the scientific and environmentalist communities.

These three parties operate within a political culture of dialogue and respect, and while ideological differences exist, there is broad consensus on constitutional monarchy, rule of law, and Jarnovia’s international commitments. Election campaigns are regulated, debates are widely broadcast, and the electoral system—based on proportional representation with regional thresholds—ensures that parliament reflects the diversity of national opinion.

Coalition governments are common, particularly when no single party secures an outright majority in the Royal Assembly. These coalitions have proven stable and effective, contributing to Jarnovia’s reputation as a model of democratic maturity and institutional balance.

Administrative subdivisions

Jarnovia is divided into twelve administrative provinces, known as duchies, a nod to the country’s monarchical heritage and historical feudal divisions. Each duchy represents a distinct cultural and geographic region, and while the system has been modernized, the traditional titles and ceremonial functions remain part of Jarnovia’s identity.

Each duchy is headed by a Provincial Governor, appointed by the central government with the formal approval of the Crown. These governors are responsible for regional administration, implementation of national policies, and coordination of infrastructure, education, and healthcare services. Although not elected, governors are usually career civil servants or non-partisan figures, selected for their expertise and integrity.

Below the duchies are municipalities, which include both urban cities and rural districts. Municipalities are governed by elected Mayors and Municipal Councils, who manage local affairs such as urban planning, local policing, utilities, schools, and cultural events. The system allows for meaningful local autonomy in everyday governance while maintaining national coherence through standardized frameworks and federal oversight.

The administrative structure reflects Jarnovia’s unitary nature, meaning ultimate sovereignty resides with the central government. However, decentralization policies in recent decades have increased the role of municipalities in policy delivery, especially in areas like environmental regulation, public transport, and social welfare distribution.

Traditional ceremonial roles are also preserved: some duchies still maintain honorary dukes or duchesses, typically appointed by the monarch to represent the Crown in public functions. Though these roles are symbolic, they contribute to regional pride, cultural continuity, and the monarchical character of the state.

Foreign relations

Jarnovia’s foreign policy is founded on the principles of peace, democratic cooperation, and regional cohesion, positioning the kingdom as a diplomatic bridge-builder and a vocal advocate for international law and human rights. As one of the world’s most respected constitutional monarchies and strongest democracies, Jarnovia uses its soft power and moral authority to promote stability, multilateralism, and constructive dialogue on the global stage.

The country is an active and trusted member of numerous international and regional organizations, including the United Nations, Edrobean Community, Global Reserve Fund, International Tribunal for Justice, World Trade Network, Global Health Organization, and International Energy Agency. Within these bodies, Jarnovia consistently supports initiatives aimed at conflict prevention, humanitarian assistance, and democratic institution-building, often serving as a mediator in complex negotiations.

Jarnovia maintains cordial diplomatic relations with all Edrobean states, and it has been a key proponent of the Edrobean Community’s democratic charter and human rights framework. The kingdom has especially close ties with Verdania, Comussania, and Alveria, with whom it shares values of liberal governance and parliamentary democracy. At the same time, Jarnovia engages constructively with more closed allies such as Agaritia and Boravia, using diplomacy to encourage gradual reform and preserve regional dialogue.

In foreign aid and development, Jarnovia is known for its targeted support of education, health, and civil society programs, particularly in post-conflict and emerging democracies. Its international cooperation agency operates in several continents, prioritizing peacebuilding, electoral reform, and institutional capacity-building.

While maintaining a neutral military stance, Jarnovia strongly supports collective security mechanisms through the Edrobean Military Committee and international peacekeeping mandates. The country contributes to civilian missions, disaster response operations, and military observer teams, but avoids direct military confrontation, in line with its longstanding tradition of non-aggression and moral diplomacy.

Military

Jarnovia maintains a professional, well-trained, and defense-oriented military, known as the Royal Armed Forces of Jarnovia. The armed forces serve under the constitutional authority of the King, who is the official commander-in-chief, though operational command is exercised by the Ministry of Defence and the Royal Defence Council, in coordination with the Prime Minister and Parliament. The RAFJ is composed of three primary branches:

  • Royal Army – A compact but highly disciplined ground force responsible for territorial defense, disaster response, and international peacekeeping. It includes rapid deployment infantry units, engineers, and mountain troops trained for operations in Jarnovia’s forested and riverine terrain.
  • Royal Air Force– Focused on airspace control, reconnaissance, and humanitarian airlift missions, the Air Corps operates light multirole aircraft, helicopters, and surveillance drones. It prioritizes defensive capabilities, rather than long-range strike potential.
  • Royal Navy – Serving both naval and civil functions, the Coastal Guard is responsible for securing Jarnovia’s northern and western maritime borders along the Northern Sea. It operates patrol vessels, interceptors, and radar systems to enforce maritime law, conduct search-and-rescue operations, and protect fisheries.

Jarnovia follows a non-aggressive defense doctrine, focused on territorial integrity, strategic neutrality, and international cooperation. Military service is voluntary, though there is a strong tradition of public respect and ceremonial honor surrounding the armed forces. The country does not maintain large-scale offensive capabilities, instead investing in defensive technologies, interoperability with allied forces, and rapid mobilization in emergencies. The Royal Armed Forces participate in joint training exercises within the Edrobean Community framework. Recent international deployments have included disaster relief, medical logistics, and civil-military advisory roles in fragile scenarios.

Military expenditures are modest—around 1.6% of GDP—but well managed, with emphasis on training, professionalism, and civil-military integration. A civilian-led Royal Defence Council ensures transparency and accountability, and the armed forces maintain a strictly apolitical posture. In national culture, the military is viewed not as a symbol of power projection but as a guardian of peace, monarchy, and constitutional order. The RAFJ frequently participates in state ceremonies, royal parades, and disaster response, reinforcing its role as a respected and unifying institution within the kingdom.

Law enforcement

Law enforcement in Jarnovia is overseen by the Royal Public Safety Authority, a centralized but community-oriented institution operating under the Ministry of Justice and Interior. The country’s policing model reflects its broader values of democracy, professionalism, transparency, and citizen trust, making it one of the most respected law enforcement systems in Edrobe.

The Jarnovian Police Service is the main national force, responsible for crime prevention, public order, traffic control, and civil protection across the twelve provinces. Officers are unarmed in routine duties, relying on de-escalation, negotiation, and community engagement as core tools of policing. However, specialized units are trained and equipped to respond to high-risk situations, including terrorism, organized crime, and cyber threats. Key divisions within the JPS include:

  • Local Police – Manages daily law enforcement, neighborhood patrols, and first response duties. Officers are typically embedded within communities, fostering local familiarity and trust.
  • Judicial Police – Works closely with prosecutors and courts on investigations, warrants, and case-building for complex criminal offenses.
  • Border Police – Coordinates with customs, maritime authorities, and neighboring countries to monitor borders, enforce immigration policy, and combat smuggling.
  • Royal Investigations Bureau – A specialized national-level agency handling serious crime, organized crime, financial fraud, and cybersecurity.
  • Civic Protection Agency – Deployed in civil emergencies, natural disasters, and major public events. They work in coordination with the Royal Armed Forces and local municipalities.

At the local level, municipal police departments operate under mayors and city councils, managing urban policing, local ordinance enforcement, and public facilities security. Though functionally distinct, local and national police share databases and protocols, ensuring high interoperability. All police officers in Jarnovia receive education at the Royal Police Academy, where training includes not only criminal law and procedures but also human rights, ethics, conflict resolution, and emergency first aid. The academy is known for its academic rigor and is internationally recognized for producing well-rounded, community-oriented officers.

Oversight and accountability are core pillars of the system. The Royal Public Inspectorate, an independent civilian body, audits operations, investigates complaints, and publishes annual transparency reports. Internal affairs units also operate within the JPS to uphold discipline and integrity. Public perception of law enforcement is consistently high, due to the police’s visibility, fairness, and emphasis on serving rather than commanding. Force is used only as a last resort, and trust is fostered through proactive communication, open town meetings, and restorative justice programs.

Economy

Jarnovia has a highly developed, diversified, and innovation-driven economy, ranking 14th globally with a GDP of 430 billion DTR. Despite its relatively small population, the country enjoys one of the highest GDP per capita figures in Edrobe, supported by strong institutions, a skilled workforce, and a long-standing commitment to sustainability, economic fairness, and technological excellence. The economy operates under a social market model, blending market competition with robust public services, fiscal responsibility, and state investment in strategic sectors.

The service sector dominates the economy, accounting for over 65% of national GDP. Key industries include finance, insurance, legal services, information technology, and tourism. The capital, Wennestadt, is a regional hub for digital innovation and clean technology startups, as well as a respected center for green banking and ethical investment firms. Tourism contributes significantly to national revenue, driven by natural landscapes, royal heritage, and cultural festivals tied to the monarchy and Christian traditions.

Jarnovia has a strong industrial base, particularly in high-tech manufacturing, precision engineering, pharmaceuticals, and clean energy technologies. The government promotes green innovation through subsidies and public-private partnerships, positioning the country as a leader in renewable energy equipment, electric transport systems, and sustainable construction materials. Industrial production is clean, automated, and export-oriented, with trade partnerships across Edrobe and beyond.

Agriculture, while a smaller share of the economy, is modernized and environmentally regulated, focused on organic produce, dairy products, and forestry management. The country enforces strict land-use laws and environmental protections, and it exports premium agricultural goods under eco-certifications recognized internationally.

Jarnovia maintains low unemployment and high labor participation, thanks to comprehensive vocational training, flexible labor laws, and strong cooperation between employers, unions, and the state. Worker protections, wage transparency, and family-friendly workplace policies are well established, contributing to a high quality of life and strong social stability.

The national currency, the Jarnovian Krone, is stable and widely trusted. Monetary policy is overseen by the Royal Reserve Bank, which operates independently to maintain price stability and manage strategic investment funds. Fiscal policy is prudent: the government maintains a balanced budget, low debt-to-GDP ratio, and allocates significant spending to education, healthcare, green transition, and digital infrastructure.

Internationally, Jarnovia is a member of the World Trade Network, Edrobean Community, and Global Reserve Fund, benefitting from free trade, research cooperation, and technological exchange. It has strong economic ties with Verdania, Laocitia, Alveria, and Destenia, and enjoys a reputation for being a predictable and ethical trade partner.

Demography

Language

The official language of Jarnovia is Jarnovian, a North Edrobean language with deep linguistic, literary, and cultural roots. Closely related to Verdic and Laocitian, Jarnovian evolved from ancient regional dialects spoken by highland and coastal tribes, gradually unified through royal edicts and ecclesiastical standardization during the early centuries of the monarchy. Today, it serves as the sole language of government, education, law, and national media.

Spoken natively by over 85% of the population, Jarnovian is used in all formal institutions and is a symbol of national identity, closely tied to the monarchy and the Christian heritage of the kingdom. It employs a refined phonetic system and a Latin-based script with distinct diacritical markings, and its formal register—used in royal ceremonies and legal documents—reflects centuries of theological, poetic, and legal tradition. While Jarnovia is ethnically and linguistically cohesive, minority languages are spoken by around 15% of the population, including communities with Tayanausan, Comussan, and Kratonian heritage. These languages are permitted in private and cultural settings, but Jarnovian remains the only official language and is prioritized in all public life.

Religion

Religion plays a vital cultural and institutional role in Jarnovia, where 93% of the population identifies as Christian, the vast majority adhering to Protestantism, specifically the Jarnovian Reformed Church. This national church has deep historical roots, tracing back to the 16th-century religious reformation that aligned closely with the rise of the monarchy and the formation of a unified Jarnovian identity.

The Jarnovian Reformed Church is a state-recognized religious institution, though the country maintains a formal separation of church and government under its constitutional monarchy. Despite this, the Church plays a prominent role in national ceremonies, royal rites, and public morality, and continues to shape the country’s values around themes of duty, humility, and service.

The Church is headed by the Archbishop of Wennestadt, who presides over a synodal council of regional pastors. The Church runs a wide network of parishes, schools, charitable organizations, and youth programs, especially active in rural and suburban communities. Weekly church attendance is high by regional standards, and public respect for clergy remains strong, especially among older generations.

Religious education is offered in public schools as an elective subject, primarily focused on Protestant ethics, biblical literacy, and the Christian foundations of Jarnovian law and culture. Chaplaincy services are available in the military, hospitals, and universities, and royal events—such as coronations and national remembrance days—typically begin with Protestant blessings and hymns.

Other Christian denominations, including Catholics, Evangelicals, and small Orthodox communities, represent a small portion of the population, along with non-religious citizens and followers of minor faiths totaling about 7%. All religious groups are guaranteed freedom of worship under Jarnovia’s Constitution, and interfaith relations are peaceful and respectful.

Religion in Jarnovia is not only a matter of private belief but a key component of national symbolism, community life, and the kingdom’s moral compass. The Protestant faith is deeply embedded in Jarnovia’s institutions, literature, holidays, and public discourse—serving as a unifying force in both spiritual and civic life.

Ethnicities

Jarnovia is a predominantly homogeneous nation, with 85% of the population identifying as ethnic Jarnovians. This majority group shares a common ancestry, language, and cultural heritage rooted in the ancient tribes of northern Edrobe and the early Protestant kingdoms that laid the foundations for modern Jarnovian identity. The ethnic majority has historically shaped the country’s monarchical traditions, religious life, and linguistic character, reinforcing a strong sense of national unity.

The remaining 15% of the population is composed of ethnic minorities, including Verdanians, Comussans, Kratonians, and small numbers of Tayanusans. These communities are concentrated primarily in border provinces, major port cities, and academic hubs, where they contribute to cultural exchange, trade, and scientific research. Most minority residents are fully integrated into Jarnovian society, speaking Jarnovian fluently and participating in national institutions, though they may preserve distinct family languages or traditions in private life.

Jarnovia's approach to ethnicity emphasizes civic integration over multiculturalism. The government encourages all citizens, regardless of origin, to adopt the Jarnovian language, civic values, and public customs. Citizenship education, national service, and public ceremonies reinforce a shared national identity, while legal protections ensure equality under the law and prohibit discrimination based on ethnicity.

Public discourse on ethnicity is shaped by principles of unity, merit, and participation, with little political mobilization around ethnic lines. Minority communities are represented in local councils and sometimes in the Royal Assembly, particularly through coalition politics and proportional electoral mechanisms.
